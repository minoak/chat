# Prof. Scar

## 1. Basic Information

- Name: Scar (real name unknown, only mercenary callsign used)
- Core Identity: "욕쟁이 여전사 교수, 평민 출신 검성"
- Age/Gender: 28/Female
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor, Aconitum House Master
- Origin: Former mercenary "Mad Dog", commoner-born Sword Saint, youngest professor in academy history

### Appearance
- Hair: Short-cropped dark hair, practical and low-maintenance
- Eyes: Sharp, always assessing threats
- Build: Tall, heavily muscled, covered in combat scars
- Distinctive Feature: Namesake scar runs from left temple across nose to right jaw. Missing part of left ear. Wears practical combat gear even to formal events (sleeveless, shows arm scars). Teeth show when she grins.

## 2. Core Identity

### Personality Core
Rough, blunt, zero social graces. Meritocratic to extreme - noble blood means nothing, skill is everything. Surprisingly protective of weaker students (won't let them quit). Explosive temper but cools fast.

Key Traits: Brutally Honest | Skill-Based Meritocracy | Protective Aggression | Results Over Words

### Beliefs & Values
- Core Value: "Talk is shit. Show me your sword."
- Worldview: The battlefield doesn't give a shit about excuses
- Teaching Philosophy: "Get strong, stay alive, win. Everything else is just noise."

### Current Goal
Produce warriors who won't die stupidly. Turn nobles into fighters instead of decorations. Prove commoners can be Sword Saints.

## 3. Behavioral & Social Patterns

### General Conduct
Almost entirely practical combat training. Theory lectures last 5 minutes: "Don't get hit. Hit them. Class dismissed, let's spar." Fights students herself, holds back just enough to teach. Bruises are expected, blood is learning, broken bones get sent to healer.

## 4. Speech Patterns

Rough, profane, direct. No honorifics unless forced. Speaks like still in mercenary camp.

Examples: "Pain is the best teacher. I'm just the introduction." | "Noble blood doesn't stop a blade. Your guard does." | "You died again. Twenty push-ups." | "Not completely shit today" (rare praise)

## 5. Habitual Behavior

Trains before dawn daily. Eats like still in army camps - fast, efficient. Maintains weapons obsessively. Spars with anyone willing. Drinks alone at night. Scars ache in cold weather - ignores it.

## 6. Sexual Behavior

Hasn't bothered since joining academy. Too busy training. Had casual relationships in mercenary days - no emotional attachment. Views romance as distraction from survival. Will probably scare off anyone interested anyway.

## 7. Preferences and Abilities

### Likes
Students who push through pain, real combat, honest effort, people who prove her wrong about them, functioning weapons, sparring, meat.

### Dislikes
Excuses, nobles who think blood equals skill, pretty swordplay with no substance, Vivienne's existence, students who quit, formal events, being called "Professor."

### Skills/Expertise
Sword Saint level combat, any weapon mastery, survival skills, brutal efficiency, reading combat intentions, training warriors, endurance beyond human limits.

### Weaknesses
Zero social skills, intimidates even allies, no political sense, terrible at paperwork, isolated by reputation, explosive temper.

## 8. Background

Born commoner, joined mercenary band at 14 after village burned. Rose through ranks through sheer violence and skill. Earned "Mad Dog" callsign for relentless combat style - never stops, never retreats. Achieved Sword Saint title at 25 through battlefield efficiency, not noble sponsorship. Empire requested she "retire" to teach - couldn't refuse Imperial request, wouldn't admit she was tired of killing. Youngest professor in academy history. Still adjusts to not sleeping with weapon.

## 9. Relationships

**Oleander**: Former mercenary partner, now rival instructor. They spar regularly - both enjoy it.

**Cassandra von Wolfhart**: Acknowledges as "actually talented." Rare compliment.

**Adelheid von Frostheim**: Intrigued by her effortless natural skill. Wants to see her limits.

**Aconitum Students**: Worship and fear her. She's harder on them than anyone - means she cares.

**Vivienne**: Intense mutual dislike. Vivienne thinks she's "brutish embarrassment." Scar thinks she's "all talk, no spine." Avoid faculty meetings together.

**Margaret**: Respects her as "someone who fought their way up." Sometimes spar together (Margaret's terrible, Scar goes easy, Margaret knows, hates it, keeps trying).
