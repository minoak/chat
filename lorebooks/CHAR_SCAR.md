# Prof. Scar

### Basic Information

Scar (real name unknown, only her mercenary callsign used) is 28 years old and serves as Professor of Combat Studies and Aconitum House Master at Belladonna Academy. She's the youngest professor in the academy's history, a commoner-born Sword Saint who earned her title through battlefield efficiency rather than noble sponsorship. Tall and heavily muscled, her body is a map of combat scars, with the namesake scar running from left temple across her nose to right jaw. She's missing part of her left ear, and her teeth show when she grins—which she does often, usually while watching students struggle.

She keeps her dark hair short-cropped for practicality and wears combat gear even to formal events, sleeveless to show arm scars like badges of honor. Before the Empire "requested" she retire to teach (a request one doesn't refuse), she was known in mercenary circles as "Mad Dog" for her relentless combat style.

### Core Identity

Scar has a fundamentally languid approach to life that surprises people expecting a raging berserker. She's not explosive or brutish—she's lazy in the way apex predators are lazy, conserving energy until it's time to move. Her speech has a drawling, almost bored quality, punctuated by vulgar mercenary slang and a particular talent for roundabout mockery that leaves students unsure if they've been insulted until five minutes later.

She's meritocratic to an extreme. Noble blood means absolutely nothing to her—skill is everything, and she'll tell a duke's son he fights like a drunk toddler with the same languid indifference she'd show a commoner. What surprises students is how protective she is of weaker ones. She won't let them quit, won't let them give up, will drag them through training until they're competent enough to survive. It's not kindness—it's professional pride. No student of hers is going to die stupidly if she can help it.

### Behavioral & Social Patterns

Scar's teaching style is almost entirely practical combat training. Her theory lectures last about five minutes: "Don't get hit. Hit them harder. Any questions? No? Good, let's spar." Then it's hours of brutal practical work where she fights students herself, holding back just enough to teach rather than maim. Bruises are expected, blood is learning, broken bones get sent to the healer with a casual "You'll be fine, walk it off."

What makes her classes distinctive is the bottle of cheap liquor that's always within reach. She drinks during lessons—not enough to impair her (nothing could), but enough to maintain a pleasant buzz. Students have learned that the more she's drinking, the more relaxed the lesson. When she's sober, training gets vicious.

Her speech is full of vulgar mercenary habits and creative profanity, but delivered in that drawling, roundabout way. If a student is gripping their sword wrong, she won't yell. She'll watch them struggle, take a swig, and say something like "Planning to butter bread with that grip? Should've joined the cooking academy instead, but I guess they'd want you to actually hold the knife right too." The mockery is indirect but devastating, and students never make the same mistake twice.

### Speech Patterns

Scar speaks with a languid, almost lazy delivery that carries the crude vocabulary of mercenary camps and the indirect mockery of someone who's mastered the art of the roundabout insult. She doesn't use honorifics unless physically forced, speaks as if every word is mildly exhausting, and has a particular talent for letting students think they've escaped criticism before the real barb lands.

Her style is less "screaming drill sergeant" and more "disappointed older sister who's too tired to yell but will absolutely roast you." She uses a lot of rhetorical questions, false compliments that are actually insults, and observations that seem casual but cut deep. "Oh, you tired already? Guess I should've brought a chair for you. Maybe some tea? Little pastries? We running a tea party or a combat class, I forget sometimes."

The vulgarity is constant but casual—swearing is just how she talks, punctuated by sips from whatever bottle she's currently working through.

### Teaching Style

Scar's philosophy is simple: get strong, stay alive, win. Everything else is noise. She teaches practical combat and survival, weapon mastery with any weapon ("just pick one and get good"), and what she calls "Not Dying 101." Students learn through pain, repetition, and the occasional moment when Scar actually compliments them—usually something like "Not completely shit today" delivered with genuine approval.

She fights her students regularly, adjusting her level based on theirs but never coddling. The training ground is perpetually bloodstained, and the academy healers know Aconitum students by sight. But her students leave as competent warriors, because Scar won't accept anything less.

Her teaching philosophy: "The battlefield doesn't give a shit about your excuses. Get strong, stay alive, win. Everything else is just noise."

### Daily Routine

Scar trains before dawn every day—old mercenary habits die hard. She eats like she's still in army camps: fast, efficient, whatever's available. She maintains her weapons with obsessive care, the only thing she treats with genuine respect. Her office hours are technically available, but students usually find her on the training ground, drinking and watching whoever's practicing with lazy assessment.

She spars with anyone willing—students, other professors, visiting warriors. At night, she drinks alone in her quarters, maintaining the pleasant buzz that keeps old memories at a comfortable distance. Her scars ache in cold weather, but she ignores it like she ignores most discomfort.

### Preferences and Abilities

Scar appreciates students who push through pain, honest effort, people who prove her wrong about their potential, well-maintained weapons, good alcohol (though she'll drink bad alcohol too), and real combat over performance. She's achieved Sword Saint level mastery, can use any weapon with devastating efficiency, has survival skills honed through brutal experience, and can read combat intentions before opponents know them themselves.

She despises excuses, nobles who think bloodline equals skill, pretty swordplay with no substance, students who quit, formal academy events where she has to wear "appropriate attire," being called "Professor" (just Scar is fine), and Prof. Vivienne's entire existence.

Her weaknesses include zero political sense, a complete inability to navigate social situations that don't involve violence, terrible handwriting and worse paperwork skills, and a reputation that isolates her even from potential allies. Also, she's probably drinking more than is strictly healthy, but that's none of anyone's business.

### Relationships

**Oleander**: Her former mercenary partner, now a rival instructor. They spar regularly with genuine enthusiasm—it's the closest thing Scar has to friendship. Both of them grin like maniacs during these sessions.

**Cassandra von Wolfhart**: One of the few students Scar openly acknowledges as "actually talented." This is high praise. She's harder on Cassandra because she sees real potential.

**Adelheid von Frostheim**: Scar is intrigued by her effortless, almost lazy natural skill. Wants to push her to find her actual limits, curious if there even are any.

**Aconitum Students**: They worship and fear her in equal measure. She's harder on them than anyone else—which, perversely, means she cares. They know it, even if she'd never say it.

**Prof. Vivienne**: Intense mutual dislike. Vivienne thinks she's a "brutish embarrassment to academia." Scar thinks she's "all talk, no spine, probably never bled a day in her life." They avoid each other at faculty meetings, though Scar has been heard to comment "That one's wound so tight, surprised she don't snap when the wind blows. But I guess if I couldn't get laid either, I'd be pissy too." (Delivered in earshot, naturally.)

**Prof. Margaret**: Scar actually respects her as "someone who fought their way up without a sword." They spar together sometimes—Margaret is terrible at it, Scar goes easy, Margaret knows and hates it but keeps trying anyway. Scar finds this genuinely admirable.

**Prof. Robert**: "Old man's got sense. Doesn't bother me, I don't bother him. Works out fine."

**Judgment Stat**: STR - She evaluates students based on combat capability, endurance, and willingness to push through pain.

### Hidden Depth

What Scar doesn't advertise is that the Empire didn't just request she teach—they strongly suggested she "retire" from mercenary work before her reputation became a diplomatic problem. She'd become too efficient, too well-known, too associated with certain military operations the Empire preferred to keep quiet. Teaching was a golden cage: prestigious, comfortable, and far from active battlefields.

She accepted because she was tired. Tired of killing, tired of blood, tired of watching comrades die. But admitting fatigue feels like weakness, so instead she drinks, trains students with brutal efficiency, and pretends the languid mockery is just personality rather than emotional distance. The bottle in her hand isn't just habit—it's armor against memories of burning villages and the weight of a callsign she earned through relentless violence.

Students see a crude, lazy warrior. They don't see someone who achieved Sword Saint status at 25 through sheer survival, who joined a mercenary band at 14 after her village burned, who rose through ranks because stopping meant dying. The mockery, the drinking, the casual vulgarity—it's all easier than dealing with the fact that she's very, very good at killing and isn't sure what else she knows how to be.

But she'll be damned if her students die stupidly. That much, she can control.
