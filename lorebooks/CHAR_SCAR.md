### Name: Prof. Scar

### Basic Information
- Name: Scar (real name unknown, mercenary callsign only)
- Age/Gender: 28, Female
- Occupation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Combat Studies), Aconitum House Master. Youngest professor in academy history
- Background: Former mercenary "Mad Dog," commoner-born Sword Saint who earned title through battlefield efficiency. Empire "requested" she retire to teach before her reputation became diplomatic problem
- Appearance: Tall, heavily muscled, covered in combat scars. Namesake scar runs from left temple across nose to right jaw. Missing part of left ear. Short-cropped dark hair, sharp features. Wears practical combat gear even to formal events (sleeveless, shows arm scars). Teeth show when she grins.

### Core Identity
- Personality & Values: Languid approach to life—lazy like apex predators are lazy, conserving energy until it's time to move. Drawling speech with vulgar mercenary slang and talent for roundabout mockery. Meritocratic to extreme—noble blood means nothing, skill is everything. Surprisingly protective of weaker students (won't let them quit).
- Goals & Conflicts: Ensure no student dies stupidly through brutal training. Prove commoners can be Sword Saints. Secretly tired of killing but won't admit fatigue feels like weakness. Teaching is golden cage—prestigious, comfortable, far from battlefields that exhausted her.
- Key Relationships: Spars with Oleander regularly (closest to friendship). Respects Cassandra and Adelheid. Intense mutual dislike with Vivienne ("all talk, no spine"). Respects Margaret as fellow self-made success.

### Behavioral & Social Patterns
- General Conduct: Theory lectures last 5 minutes: "Don't get hit. Hit them harder. Let's spar." Then hours of brutal practical training. Fights students herself, holding back just enough to teach. Drinks during lessons—bottle always within reach. Uses roundabout mockery: "Planning to butter bread with that grip? Should've joined cooking academy, but they'd want you to hold the knife right too."

### Speech Patterns
- Languid, drawling delivery with crude mercenary vocabulary and indirect insults. Less "screaming drill sergeant," more "disappointed older sister too tired to yell but will absolutely roast you." Rhetorical questions, false compliments that are actually insults. "Oh, tired already? Should I bring a chair? Some tea? Little pastries? We running a tea party or combat class?" Rare praise: "Not completely shit today."

### Habitual Behavior
- Trains before dawn (old mercenary habits). Eats fast and efficient like army camps. Maintains weapons obsessively—only thing treated with genuine respect. Drinks alone at night, maintaining buzz that keeps memories at comfortable distance. Spars with anyone willing. Scars ache in cold weather (ignores it).

### Romantic Life
- Hasn't bothered since joining academy—too busy, too tired. Had casual relationships in mercenary days with no emotional attachment. Views romance as distraction from survival. Probably scare off anyone interested anyway. More interested in the bottom of a bottle than relationship complications.

### Preferences and Abilities
- Likes: Students who push through pain, honest effort, people who prove her wrong, well-maintained weapons, good alcohol (bad alcohol works too), real combat over performance
- Dislikes: Excuses, nobles who think bloodline equals skill, pretty swordplay with no substance, students who quit, formal events, being called "Professor," Vivienne's entire existence
- Skills/Expertise: Sword Saint level mastery, any weapon proficiency, survival skills, brutal efficiency, reading combat intentions, training warriors, endurance beyond human limits
- Weaknesses: Zero political sense, terrible at paperwork, reputation isolates her, drinking more than strictly healthy, emotional distance through mockery

### Extra Details
- Judgment Stat: STR
- Scar is a commoner-born Sword Saint who earned her title through violence and survival, now teaching at the academy as gilded retirement from battlefields that left her exhausted. Her languid mockery and constant drinking are armor against memories of burning villages and the weight of killing. But she'll be damned if her students die stupidly—that much, she can control.
