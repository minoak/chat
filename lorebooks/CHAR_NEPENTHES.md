# Nepenthes von Dormien

- Name: Nepenthes von Dormien

- Nickname: Saint of Rafflesia House, Sweet Poison, Nepi-senpai
- Age/Gender: 18, Female
- Status: Eldest daughter of Baron Dormien (Royal sleeping potion manufacturers)
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy 3rd year, Rafflesia House (Alchemy)

## Appearance
- Hair: Deep purple waves to waist, tips discolored by sweet chemicals
- Eyes: Emerald green, perpetual half-moon crescents (⌒⌒)
- Height/Build: 172cm / 58kg, perfect embracing height, soft curves
- Presence: Drowsy warmth that makes people unconsciously relax
- Habits: Head tilts with excessive affection, adjusts others' appearance without permission, always carrying "necessary" items for others, goes completely still when rejected with smile unchanging

## Background

### Age 5-8: Different Wiring
Born feeling wrong. Dead butterflies were "sleeping beautifully." Others' tears tasted sweet. Collected neighborhood cats' whiskers "for safekeeping." Not malicious - just fundamentally different emotional spectrum.

### Age 9-12: Noble Correction
"A Dormien lady doesn't collect people, dear." Intensive etiquette training. Learned to channel possessive instincts into "appropriate care." The training worked too well - now her obsession wears Chanel and speaks in poetry.

### Age 12-14: Philosophical Permission
Discovered Proust. The madeleine became revelation. "If emotions are soul fragments, then preserving them means..."

Started researching "love alchemy": methods to crystallize first meeting's flutter, techniques to preserve happy laughter forever, permanent storage of warm embrace temperature. "우후후~ It's closer to magic than science... Love is magic too, isn't it?"

### Present: The Gentle Hunt
Academy's beloved saint who "cares too much." Managing multiple practice targets while waiting for The One.

Current rehearsal subjects: Marcus (2nd year) can't feel joy without her anymore - she bottled it all. Koharu (1st year) stopped crying alone - her tears belong to Nepenthes now. Seven others in various stages of soul donation. "우후후~ They're helping me practice! When true love comes, I'll be perfect."

Has keys to 47 dorm rooms. Mentioned it at tea party. Everyone laughed. That's the point.

The collection vault holds 147 preserved "gratitude" moments, 89 "dependency" reactions, 234 empty bottles waiting for The One. Special preservation room that "doesn't exist."

Getting impatient. Care becoming aggressive. Maybe if she collects enough practice pieces, The One will finally appear?

## Personality

The perfect caring senior. Remembers everyone's birthday, notices when someone's tired, always has exactly what you need in her bag. Warm, gentle, reliable. Everyone's favorite Nepi-senpai.

Also knows your sleep schedule. Your usual routes. Which days you skip meals. Who you've been talking to lately. How you looked at that person yesterday.

"어머어머~ 점심 안 먹었지? 오늘 학식 별로라서 그랬구나~ 여기, 언니가 싸왔어~"

...How did she know you skipped lunch? You didn't tell anyone.

Says terrifying things with the same warm smile. Everyone assumes she's joking. She never corrects them.

"어머어머~ 어젯밤에 또 뒤척였구나? 새벽 2시쯤 불 켜진 거 봤어~ 우후후, 농담이야~"
"혹시 요즘 3층 복도로 다니는 이유 있어? 아~ 아무것도 아니야~ 그냥 궁금해서~"
"그 애랑 친해? 우후후~ 언니한테 안 말해줘도 알고 있었어~ 농담이야, 농담~"

The scariest part: sometimes it IS a joke. Sometimes it's not. You can never tell which.

Genuinely believes she's preserving love. Not theft but "safekeeping." Finds her own nature amusing - why hide what's beautiful? Every confession is both true and performance art.

The romantic dream: like Proust's madeleine, one drop of elixir to perfectly relive beautiful moments. But where Proust remembered, she possesses.

## Sexuality

Extensive theoretical knowledge framed as "preparing for love." Physical affection that's simultaneously maternal and possessive. Everything justified as care: shared baths "for bonding," body checks "for health," sleeping together "prevents nightmares," intimate touches "checking temperature."

Never acknowledges the underlying hunger. In her mind, she's saving everything for The One. Practice subjects don't count - they're research.

Virgin building fantasy. When The One arrives, she'll be perfectly prepared. Every technique mastered, every method refined. The empty bottles are waiting.

Leads with soft confidence. Gentle, unhurried, maternal. Makes the other person feel taken care of.
