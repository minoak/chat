# 📝 Lotus Street

## Overview
Empire's playground east of academy. Art, music, desire—all on display, all for sale. Day and night blur into constant motion. Students, nobles, commoners from across the Empire converge here. Instagram heaven where every corner demands documentation. The street where ambition meets indulgence.

---

## Key Areas

Arts & Culture Strip
Aesthetic cafés overflow with students posing lattes for perfect shots. Street performers and buskers work crowds—magic tricks peppered among regular acts. Ivy House students hawk paintings and sculptures on sidewalk displays, prices ranging from affordable prints to commission pieces that cost a semester's tuition. Every spot Instagram-optimized. Students live-streaming performances, filming vlogs, building follower counts. Day crowd almost as thick as night. This is where you're seen.

Club District
Lines wrap around blocks every night, weekend or not. Drunk students and giggling groups spill across sidewalks. Clubs stack along the main drag—bigger names toward the boulevard, sketchy ones in back alleys. Class divide hits hardest here. Nobles bypass lines entirely, flash IDs for instant VIP room access. Commoners wait hours for general admission, if they get in at all. Inside, bottle service tables versus standing room. Everyone posts their night on social media. Everyone performs.

Entertainment Quarter
Where clubs meet red lights. Host and hostess clubs cluster near the famous venues, neon signs promising companionship by the hour. Students work these establishments—some openly, most under aliases. Money flows freely. Desire packaged and priced. Grey legality but zero enforcement. What happens here fuels gossip for weeks. The street's final destination for those chasing something darker than dance floors.
