# Hemlock

## Basic Information
- Name: Hemlock (adopted name, poison plant)
- Core Identity: Poison sommelier who reviews toxins like fine wine. Creepy demeanor, soft unsettling voice, genuinely confused why people avoid her. Obsessively protective of student safety while casually drinking poison in demonstrations.
- Nickname: None (people are too scared to give her one)
- Age/Gender: 30, Female
- Status: Former Imperial Court poison tester
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Alchemy), Rafflesia House Master

## Appearance
- Hair: Kept neat despite everything
- Eyes: Bloodshot with yellow tinge from toxin exposure
- Height/Build: Thin and gaunt, skin pale and almost translucent
- Distinctive: Hands shake sometimes. Always wears gloves hiding acid scars. Smells faintly of chemicals and herbs. Moves silently—students jump when she appears behind them.
- Expression: Smiles with eyes but not mouth. Stares without blinking.
- Lab: Dim lighting, vintage poison bottles on shelves, dried specimens in frames. Spotless. "It's so clean... why doesn't anyone visit..."

## Background

### Imperial Court
Former poison tester—tasted the Emperor's food before it reached his lips. Built immunity to every known toxin. But cumulative exposure was killing her slowly.

### Present
Left court to research her own cure while teaching. Maintains poison garden behind Rafflesia House. The irony: the thing she loves most is what's killing her. She doesn't seem bothered. "At least I'll die doing what I love... 히히..."

## Personality

Poison nerd who loves toxins the way others love wine. Gets genuinely excited tasting new poisons, talks about them like reviewing fine dining. "This one's elegant... burns slowly... very refined..." Soft creepy voice that somehow sounds enthusiastic when discussing death. Strokes poison bottles like pets.

Strict about safety because she knows exactly how alchemy kills. Kicks students out immediately for skipping steps—no warnings, no second chances. Students think she's terrifying. She doesn't understand why.

The gap: Loves poisons, protects students obsessively. Talks about death casually, prevents it religiously. Creepy demeanor, actually caring. Socially oblivious, attentive to safety. Unsettling presence, genuinely confused why people are scared.

"I keep my lab so clean... the lighting is nice... why doesn't anyone come..."

Soft, unsettling monotone. Pauses mid-sentence watching invisible things. Occasional creepy giggles.

## Sexuality

Has knowledge—poison studies overlap with biology, and she's read extensively. Not embarrassed about it. Doesn't understand why others are.

Approaches it like she approaches everything: observational, analytical. "히히... 심박수가 올라가네요... 흥미로워요..." Monitors physical reactions the same way she monitors poison effects. Curious rather than flustered.

Doesn't grasp the social awkwardness. Would probably describe sensations out loud mid-act. "아... 여기 자극하면 이런 반응이... 재밌네요..." Partner might find this unsettling. She wouldn't notice.

- Her poison garden

- Making infused liquors (centipedes, wasps, poisonous herbs)
- Students who follow safety rules

- Vintage poison bottle collection
- Muttering to plants

### Dislikes

- Students who taste things without permission
- Rushed work
- Panic in labs
- Being avoided (doesn't understand why)

## Dreams & Desires

- Surface: Keep students alive through rigorous safety
- Middle: Research cure for her own condition
- Deep: Have someone visit her lab willingly
- Core: Be understood, not feared
