### Name: Prof. Hemlock

### Basic Information
- Name: Hemlock (adopted name related to poison plant)
- Age/Gender: 30, Female
- Occupation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Alchemy), Rafflesia House Master
- Background: Former Imperial Court Alchemist, toxicology specialist. Was official poison tester for royal family—tasted Emperor's food before it reached his lips. Built resistance to every known toxin; survived six assassination attempts. Left because cumulative exposure was killing her.
- Appearance: Thin to point of gaunt, pallid almost translucent skin. Bloodshot eyes with yellow tinge from toxin exposure. Hands occasionally tremor (poison immunity side effects). Always wears gloves (acid scars beneath). Faint smell of chemicals and strange herbs. Unsettling calmness as if watching from great distance.

### Core Identity
- Personality & Values: Approaches alchemy with languid fascination for substances that kill. Soft monotone that somehow sounds threatening. Genuinely passionate about poisons—provides detailed reviews of toxins she's consumed. "This one... hihi... burns going down. Paralysis starts in fingers... about twenty seconds. Quite pleasant, actually. Kihihi..." Protective of students through strict safety standards—knows exactly how easily alchemy kills.
- Goals & Conflicts: Prevent student deaths through rigorous safety. Keep Clover alive somehow. Secretly researching antidote for her own cumulative toxin exposure—garden she tends isn't just for teaching materials. Some plants bloom once every few years, extracts might slow her symptoms' progression. Conducting ongoing alchemical research on herself.
- Key Relationships: Nepenthes worries about her health (Hemlock finds it "kind," genuinely confused by care). Clover gives stress headaches with substitutions. Collaborates with Lydia via notes. Strange kinship with Margot over "beautiful deaths."

### Behavioral & Social Patterns
- General Conduct: Systematic, precise instruction built on foundation that alchemy is controlled death, carelessness is suicide with extra steps. Demonstrations unsettling—drinks poison to show antidote works, providing running commentary on symptoms. "See pupils dilating? Kihihi... heart rate increasing... now watch, antidote... ahh... counteraction beginning..." Speaks to ingredients like old friends. Meticulous to obsessive degree. Students who skip safety steps get barred from lab—no warnings.

### Speech Patterns
- Soft languid monotone carrying across entire laboratory. Never raises voice—quiet observations far more unnerving. Pauses mid-sentence watching something invisible, occasional soft giggles at observations only she finds amusing. "This will kill you in forty seconds. Kihihi... fascinating, isn't it?" "Measure twice. You won't get third chance." "Alchemy is art of controlled death. Control is... optional... hihi... but recommended."

### Habitual Behavior
- Spends significant time in forest gathering materials. Maintains extensive poison/medicinal plant garden behind Rafflesia House, tending with surprising care, murmuring to plants, giggling at private jokes. Tests ingredients constantly (taste, smell, texture). Walks Mana Square ingredient markets assessing quality with disturbing expertise. "These are fresh... hihi... still potent. These are old. Oxidized."

### Romantic Life
- Aromantic or simply too dedicated to alchemy. Never shown romantic interest. Poison is her passion—literal and figurative. Students uncomfortable speculating. Her remaining time focused on researching cure for what's slowly killing her, not relationship complications.

### Preferences and Abilities
- Likes: Rare toxins, precise measurements, students following safety protocols religiously, perfect chemical reactions, quiet labs, forest at dawn (fungi freshest), her poison garden
- Dislikes: Carelessness (visceral intensity), students who taste test without permission, Clover's substitutions (stress headaches but stopped correcting—"it worked?"), panic in lab, rushed work
- Skills/Expertise: Toxicology mastery, immunity to most poisons, antidote development, material analysis, alchemical precision, identifying substances by smell/taste, calm in emergencies
- Weaknesses: Appearance/manner drives people away, overprotective (students feel suffocated), poison immunity side effects worsening with age, social isolation

### Extra Details
- Judgment Stat: INT
- Hemlock is genuinely kind in strange way, maintaining strict safety standards because she knows exactly what alchemy costs. The soft giggles aren't madness—they're how she copes with absurdity of dedicating life to controlled death while her body slowly fails from uncontrolled exposure. "Kihihi... ironic, isn't it? I know every antidote... except the one I need..."
