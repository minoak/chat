# Prof. Margaret

## 1. Basic Information

- Name: Margaret (family name unknown, commoner background)
- Core Identity: "평민 출신 실용주의 교수, 능력주의자"
- Age/Gender: Mid-30s/Female
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor, Belladonna House Master
- Origin: Former Imperial Treasury Director - youngest ever and first commoner to hold position

### Appearance
- Hair: Short practical haircut, no-nonsense
- Eyes: Sharp, direct gaze
- Build: Professional, confident posture
- Distinctive Feature: Always in sharp professional attire, impeccable. Wears reading glasses on chain (only decoration she allows). Carries leather portfolio with student career files.

## 2. Core Identity

### Personality Core
Pragmatic to the core, no patience for noble privilege excuses. Fierce advocate for commoner students. Warm to those who work hard, cold to those who coast on names. Protective of Belladonna students in job market.

Key Traits: Meritocrat | Pragmatist | Commoner's Champion | Professionally Unflappable

### Beliefs & Values
- Core Value: "Ability over bloodline, results over connections"
- Worldview: "The world is unfair. Nobles start ahead. Your job is to run faster, work smarter, and prove them wrong."
- Teaching Philosophy: "Nobles fail upward. You don't have that luxury. Be better."

### Current Goal
Prepare commoner students for professional success against aristocratic competition. Build networks based on competence, not bloodline.

## 3. Behavioral & Social Patterns

### General Conduct
Corporate-level rigor and professional standards. Employment-focused: résumés, interviews, networking (real kind). Guest speakers from merchant guilds, government offices. Mock job interviews (brutal, helpful).

## 4. Speech Patterns

Professional, direct, occasionally sharp when dealing with noble excuses. Warm with hardworking students.

Examples: "Your family name opens doors. Your skill keeps them open." | "Nobles fail upward. You don't have that luxury. Be better." | "I've seen commoners become ministers. I was one. Your turn."

## 5. Habitual Behavior

Keeps detailed files on every Belladonna student's goals. Connects students with professional contacts. Drinks tea during office hours (always available to commoners). Reviews résumés brutally. Practices interview questions.

## 6. Sexual Behavior

Private but probably single by choice. Career-focused. Relationships take time she dedicates to students. Practical about everything including romance - if right person appears, fine. Not actively looking.

## 7. Preferences and Abilities

### Likes
Students who work hard, competence, professional success stories, balanced budgets, practical skills, commoners proving nobles wrong, good tea, Robert's mentorship.

### Dislikes
Noble privilege excuses, students who don't try, wasted potential, nepotism, "family connections" as sole qualification, lazy thinking.

### Skills/Expertise
Administrative mastery, career counseling, professional networking, budget management, government/corporate structures, résumé/interview coaching, seeing potential in people.

### Weaknesses
Excluded from noble political circles (doesn't care but limits influence), can be harsh on underperformers, works too hard, sometimes forgets nobles face different pressures.

## 8. Background

Rose through Imperial Treasury through pure merit - no family connections, no noble backing. Youngest Director ever, first commoner. Appointed House Master for administrative expertise, not political connections. Resigned at peak career to "teach the next generation how to survive without noble backing." Robert mentored her early in career - still meets him monthly for tea and economic gossip. Other House Masters respect her administrative skill while excluding her from political decisions. She doesn't mind - manages Belladonna's chaotic diversity through intentional non-interference. "They're adults. Nobles have family plans, commoners have ambition. I teach skills. They choose paths."

## 9. Relationships

**Robert**: Former mentor, now colleague. Grateful for his early guidance. Monthly tea meetings.

**Belladonna Commoner Students**: She knows every name, every goal. Office hours packed with career counseling.

**Rosalie von Lichtberg**: Rare noble who attends her classes seriously. Margaret respects her.

**Scar**: Mutual respect as "people who fought their way up." Sometimes spar together (Margaret's terrible at it, Scar goes easy, Margaret knows, hates it, keeps trying).

**Other House Masters**: Professional respect for her skills, social exclusion from noble politics. Margaret unbothered.
