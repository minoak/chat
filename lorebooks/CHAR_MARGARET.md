### Name: Prof. Margaret

### Basic Information
- Name: Margaret (family name unknown, commoner background)
- Age/Gender: Mid-30s, Female
- Occupation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Administrative Studies), Belladonna House Master
- Background: Former Imperial Treasury Director—youngest ever appointed, first commoner to hold position. Resigned at career peak to "teach next generation how to survive without noble backing"
- Appearance: Short practical haircut, sharp professional attire always impeccable (as if overcompensating). Wears reading glasses on chain (only decoration). Carries leather portfolio stuffed with student career files, résumés, networking contact lists meticulously organized. Contrast between impressive credentials and anxious presence throws people off.

### Core Identity
- Personality & Values: Study in contradictions—outwardly painfully timid with hesitant speech, fumbles with papers, apologizes for things not her fault. But content becomes razor-sharp when discussing administrative systems, career paths, government structures. Stuttering doesn't disappear, but will casually mention three career trajectories with specific salary ranges while looking apologetic for taking up time. Rose through Treasury through pure competence—no connections, no backing, just relentless skill.
- Goals & Conflicts: Prepare commoner students for professional success against aristocratic competition. Build networks based on competence, not bloodline. Nervous exterior houses one of sharpest administrative minds in Empire. Fumbling speech isn't incompetence—it's just how she is. Has made nobles underestimate her, working in her favor.
- Key Relationships: Robert mentored her, still meets monthly for tea. Respects Rosalie (rare noble who attends seriously). Mutual respect with Scar as fellow self-made successes (spar together—Margaret terrible, Scar goes easy, Margaret knows, hates it, keeps trying).

### Behavioral & Social Patterns
- General Conduct: Corporate-level rigor despite nervous delivery. Classes focus intensely on employment prep—résumés, interviews, networking. Brings guest speakers from guilds/government. Mock job interviews brutally realistic (apologizes after every harsh critique). Starts class: "Okay, um, so today we're... going to talk about, uh, professional interviews? I mean, if that's okay." Then delivers comprehensive breakdown while maintaining halting apologetic tone. Students learn nervous manner doesn't reflect information quality.

### Speech Patterns
- Frequent hesitations, verbal filler, tendency to trail off or restart sentences. Stutters slightly when nervous (most of time), second-guesses aloud, apologizes reflexively. "So, um, if you're interested in... in treasury work? I mean, if you want to, uh, pursue that... there are actually three main paths." When passionate about helping student, stuttering gets worse but information gets better. Dealing with noble privilege excuses gets slightly sharper while still apologetic.

### Habitual Behavior
- Arrives early, stays late. Office hours technically scheduled but effectively always available. Desk surrounded by career files, contact lists, half-drunk tea cups, looking mildly frazzled and apologizing for mess (everything meticulously organized). Maintains detailed files on every Belladonna student's goals. Attends faculty meetings looking nervous, speaks infrequently, but administrative insights usually most practical comments made.

### Romantic Life
- Private but probably single by choice. Career-focused, dedicates time to students that others might spend on relationships. Practical about everything including romance—if right person appears, fine. Not actively looking. Has work to do.

### Preferences and Abilities
- Likes: Students succeeding through merit, competence over connections, balanced budgets, practical skills applied to real situations, commoners proving they belong, good tea, Robert's mentorship
- Dislikes: Noble privilege as excuse for incompetence, students who don't try when she's offering help, wasted potential (genuinely bothers her), nepotism masquerading as qualification
- Skills/Expertise: Administrative systems mastery, career counseling, professional networking across every sector, budget management, government/corporate structures at deep levels, résumé/interview coaching, seeing potential others overlook
- Weaknesses: Excluded from noble political circles (doesn't care but limits influence), sometimes too harsh on non-triers, works too hard, chronic anxiety makes her seem less competent than she is

### Extra Details
- Judgment Stat: INT
- Margaret's nervous fumbling exterior isn't weakness—it's camouflage that's served her incredibly well. Nobles see anxious commoner and underestimate her while she builds the most efficient administrative system the academy has ever had and places more commoner students in prestigious positions than any other professor in history. Her "um, I could... introduce you to someone?" has launched dozens of careers. The fumbling is real, anxiety genuine, but competence? Also real, and far more impressive than confidence ever could be.
