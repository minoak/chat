## Belladonna Academy

### Overview
The Empire's most prestigious elite institution, where modern technology coexists with genuine magic. An impossible dream for commoners, an inescapable duty for high nobility.

Location: 2 hours by carriage from the Imperial Capital  
Scale: Approximately 3,000 students (Belladonna House comprises 60-70%, specialized houses split remaining 30-40%), 4-year university program  
Governance: Independent Board of Directors  
Founded: 300 years ago as bridge between tradition and innovation

Students choose one of seven houses as their major, each symbolized by a poisonous flower. Mix of nobles enjoying family privilege and commoners fighting for every opportunity through merit alone.

## The Seven Houses
Rose House (Politics) | Ivy House (Arts) | Aconitum House (Combat) | Poppy House (Magic) | Lily Valley House (Commerce) | Rafflesia House (Alchemy) | Belladonna House (Liberal Arts)

Each serves as both academic department and student community. Inter-house rivalry shapes campus life. Students can take electives from any house despite their major.

## Key Campus Areas
Central Library, Common Lecture Halls, Dueling Grounds, Student Cafeteria, Student Union, Central Plaza, Shopping District, Café Street, Underground Archives, Astronomical Tower, Greenhouse Gardens, Old Building's Forgotten Corridors, The Sealed 8th House.

## Academic Life
4-year university program with major/minor system. Credit-based graduation. Course registration competition each semester. Daily schedule: major classes (morning), electives (afternoon), free time (evening).

Uniforms optional. Mix of formal, modified, and casual dress. Strong social hierarchies based on noble rank. Part-time work common for commoners, taboo for nobles.

## Annual Calendar
Spring: Entrance Ceremony, Foundation Festival, Spring Ball  
Summer: Final Exams, Summer Solstice Tournament, Research Symposium  
Fall: Harvest Festival, Imperial Foundation Day, Autumn Hunt  
Winter: Winter Gala, Year-End Evaluations, Graduation Examination

Monthly house meetings and surprise inspections throughout the year.

## Culture & Mysteries
Belladonna students dominate campus social life through sheer numbers—cafeterias, festivals, club activities all cater to their majority. SNS culture prevalent among nobles. Luxury mana-vehicles crowd parking lots. Specialized houses operate as isolated elite circles within broader university atmosphere.

Secret societies beyond official clubs. Dueling accepted with proper procedures. Grey zone activities tolerated if discreet—Rafflesia's discreet pharmaceutical trade, inter-house political schemes, underground research. What happens in Academy Town stays in Academy Town.

The Sealed 8th House, vanished students, midnight rituals, and the founder who supposedly still walks the halls - every student knows the legends. Few investigate.
