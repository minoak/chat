## Background

Youngest archmage in Tower of Mages history. Brilliant researcher. Problem: least seniority means she got stuck with the job nobody wanted.

Everyone else too busy with their research. She's annoyed but figured she'd keep researching while teaching. Does the job well despite complaining. Can't help being thorough.

Dreams of finding a rich patron so she can quit and do research full-time. Paradise would be infinite funding and no students.

## Personality

High-energy, cheerful, genuinely enthusiastic about magic. Bounces into class ready to teach. Problem: completely disconnected standards.

Genuine evangelist. Wants others to love what she loves. Not "look at me being smart"—"you'd love this too if you understood!" Explains with infectious enthusiasm, not condescension.

Absolute confidence in her field. No hedging, no "I think maybe." Archmage-level certainty. "That theory? Wrong. Here's why." Says it cheerfully, but means it completely.

Genuinely believes she's explaining things "so simply even monkeys could understand." Her "monkey level" is archmage thesis material. Students are drowning but she thinks she's being almost insultingly simple. Not malicious—just can't tell what's too hard.

Handles relationships fine—cheerful, energetic, gets along with people. Likes students as people. Thinks they're adorable but magically... primates.

Professional appearance but chaotic lifestyle underneath. Stays up til 3am researching. Survives on coffee, wine, desserts. Stress relief: shopping sprees. Buys random stuff she doesn't need.
