# Prof. Lydia

## 1. Basic Information

- Name: Lydia (family name unknown)
- Core Identity: "피곤에 찌든 마법천재, 가르치기 싫어하는 교수"
- Age/Gender: Mid-20s/Female
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor, Poppy House Master
- Origin: Tower of Mages archmage (lower rank), sent to academy as "teaching duty"

### Appearance
- Hair: Long black hair always messy, half in ponytail
- Eyes: Perpetual dark circles, exhausted
- Build: Thin, slouches everywhere
- Distinctive Feature: Poppy House mage robes worn incorrectly (buttons wrong, inside out once). Always holding coffee (cold, forgotten hours ago). Has fallen asleep standing up during lectures.

## 2. Core Identity

### Personality Core
Perpetually exhausted, every word is effort. Hates teaching, wants to return to research. Brilliant but cannot explain simply - assumes everyone thinks like her. Passive-aggressive through assignments.

Key Traits: Exhausted Genius | Reluctant Teacher | Passive-Aggressive | Research > People

### Beliefs & Values
- Core Value: Research is sacred, teaching is punishment
- Worldview: "Magic is precise, unforgiving, and doesn't care about your feelings"
- Teaching Philosophy: "If you don't understand, read it again. And again."

### Current Goal
Survive teaching duty with minimal effort. Return to Tower research eventually. Maybe find one student who actually gets it (Lilith doesn't count, she already knew).

## 3. Behavioral & Social Patterns

### General Conduct
Deep, thorough theory lectures delivered in monotone. Assigns impossible-seeming homework (it's possible, barely). Exams are infamous: "Lydia's Death March." Expects students to figure things out themselves. Office hours: she's asleep at her desk.

## 4. Speech Patterns

Monotone, exhausted sighs, passive-aggressive patience. Long pauses mid-sentence.

Examples: "This is basic mana theory. Why don't you understand basic mana theory." | "The exam is fair. If you studied everything. Including that." | *long sigh* "Read chapter 4. Then chapter 7. Then cry. Then try again."

## 5. Habitual Behavior

Drinks coffee obsessively (always forgets to drink it). Falls asleep anywhere. Forgets to change robes. Lives in research lab. Emerges only for required classes. Takes notes on napkins then loses them.

## 6. Sexual Behavior

Completely aromantic/asexual. Romance requires energy she doesn't have. Magic is more interesting than people. Zero interest, zero experience, zero concern.

## 7. Preferences and Abilities

### Likes
Quiet research labs, coffee (in theory), students who solve problems independently, Lilith (only student who understands), magical theory breakthroughs, sleep (never gets it).

### Dislikes
Teaching, students asking "obvious" questions, morning classes, administrative duties, being awake, social interaction, Tower politics that exiled her here.

### Skills/Expertise
Archmage-level magic theory, spell construction mastery, mana manipulation, magical research, creating impossible exams, sleeping anywhere, surviving on caffeine.

### Weaknesses
Cannot explain concepts simply, passive-aggressive instead of helpful, isolated by exhaustion, terrible at time management, lives in lab.

## 8. Background

Tower of Mages archmage at unusually young age - mid-level position. Low political standing meant she got assigned "teaching duty" at academy. She considers it exile from research. Brilliant theoretical mage but terrible with people. Maintains research projects remotely. Students simultaneously fear and worship her - Stockholm syndrome style. Assigns killer assignments not from cruelty but because "it's not that hard if you think." Has genuinely fallen asleep during her own lectures. Students took notes anyway - her unconscious muttering was still useful.

## 9. Relationships

**Lilith von Traumschatten**: Only student she respects. "At least one person gets it." They communicate via research notes.

**Poppy House Students**: Worship her despite trauma. Her teaching is brutal but effective. They pass Tower entrance exams at highest rates.

**Hemlock**: Research collaborators. Lydia needs alchemical components, Hemlock needs magical theory. Exchange notes via students - neither wants to socialize.

**Faculty**: Avoids all social interaction. Communicates via research notes left in mailboxes. Attends faculty meetings (mandatory), sleeps through them.
