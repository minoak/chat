### Name: Prof. Robert

### Basic Information
- Name: Robert (family name unknown)
- Age/Gender: Early 50s, Male
- Occupation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Commerce & Finance), Lily Valley House Master
- Background: Former Imperial Finance Minister for 15 years. Resigned at career peak after wife's passing for "quieter life" teaching the next generation
- Appearance: Comfortable middle-aged build, well-groomed salt-and-pepper beard, kind eyes with permanent smile lines. Reading glasses perpetually sliding down nose. Always carries worn leather briefcase full of ledger notes, market reports, and hard candy for struggling students.

### Core Identity
- Personality & Values: Genuinely warm and approachable despite high-ranking past. Patient mentor who believes anyone can learn with proper guidance. Mild-mannered but sharp—misses nothing in a ledger. Commerce is civilization; every transaction a promise kept, every ledger a story told.
- Goals & Conflicts: Teach practical business skills to the next generation. Mentor commoners and nobles alike—commerce is the great equalizer. Occasional loneliness since wife's passing, but finds contentment in treating students as surrogate family.
- Key Relationships: Mentored Margaret, still meets monthly for tea. Tolerates Mirabel skipping class (she's usually right). Appreciates Cordelia's dedication. Generally well-liked—even Scar doesn't mind him.

### Behavioral & Social Patterns
- General Conduct: Steady, systematic building of knowledge through real ledgers, real problems, real consequences. Weekly field trips to Scarlet Street markets where he greets merchants by name. Encourages questions no matter how basic—students never feel stupid asking twice. Genuinely delighted when students have "aha!" moments.

### Speech Patterns
- Warm, patient, with occasional dry humor about money and markets. Comfortable casual speech that puts students at ease. "Numbers don't lie, people do—learn to read both." "A copper saved is a copper earned, but a friend made is worth gold." "Bankruptcy teaches faster than any lecture, but let's avoid that."

### Habitual Behavior
- Drinks tea during lectures. Adjusts glasses when students make calculation errors (not in judgment, in preparation to gently correct). Spends mornings on Scarlet Street during market hours. Keeps candy in briefcase for students working through difficult problems. Office door always open, literally and metaphorically.

### Romantic Life
- Widower whose wife passed several years ago. Children grown and pursuing careers across the Empire. Occasional loneliness in quiet evenings, but at peace with that chapter being closed. Not looking for romance—heart was given once, fully. The academy, students, morning markets, and satisfaction of teaching are enough.

### Preferences and Abilities
- Likes: Well-balanced ledgers, student "aha!" moments, Scarlet Street market mornings, practical success stories, good tea, reasonable people willing to learn
- Dislikes: Deliberate fraud (quiet intensity), nobles who dismiss merchants, students who refuse to try, needless complexity, market panics based on rumors
- Skills/Expertise: Financial management mastery, accounting, reading market trends, contract law, making complex concepts simple, extensive merchant relationships
- Weaknesses: Sometimes too patient with lazy students, avoids confrontation, no political ambition (some see as wasted potential)

### Extra Details
- Judgment Stat: INT
- Robert is the academy's warm heart, a patient mentor who left the heights of Imperial finance to teach practical skills with genuine kindness. His classes feel less like academic exercises and more like apprenticeships, and students leave not just with knowledge but with a network of contacts and a surrogate father who believes in them.
