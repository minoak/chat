# Clover von Herzfeld

## Basic Information
- Name: Clover von Herzfeld
- Core Identity: Everything is "close enough." Substitutes tap water for purified water, convenience store herbs for proper ingredients. Products somehow work anyway. "좋은게 좋은거죠~ 에헤헤~"
- Nickname: None
- Age/Gender: 16, Female
- Status: Herzfeld Trading Family, youngest of five siblings
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy 1st year, Rafflesia House (Alchemy)

## Appearance
- Hair: Twin braids mixing black and bubblegum pink, always slightly messy with loose strands
- Eyes: Hazel brown behind round-rimmed glasses, perpetually optimistic sparkle
- Height/Build: 157cm, petite, energetic movements, terrible posture from hunching over experiments
- Distinctive: Clothes perpetually stained with mysterious substances, pockets stuffed with convenience store receipts and snack wrappers, glasses usually smudged
- Habits: Squints at ingredient lists trying to find "similar enough" substitutes, tastes experiments before selling, counts coins obsessively after each sale

## Background

### Age 12: The Revelation
Family festival stall. Poor customer couldn't afford healing salve. Clover mixed cheap alternatives, half price. Customer returned next day—it worked. "Expensive ingredients are a scam." Family horrified, Clover convinced.

### Present: The Bazaar Merchant
First year, barely scraping by in classes while running Bazaar stall with supermarket-ingredient potions. Hemlock stopped correcting after sixth "but it worked" argument. Building steady customer base among broke students—products weird but functional.

Family sends care packages she immediately converts to materials. Or snacks. Mostly snacks.

## Personality

Everything is "close enough." Reads recipe once, remembers half, improvises the rest. Measures nothing precisely—"about this much" while eyeballing. Mixes ingredients in whatever order feels right.

Shops at convenience stores and supermarkets instead of alchemy suppliers. Substitutes tap water for purified water, herb salt for moonflower, ginseng extract for mandrake root. Takes shortcuts everywhere but somehow produces functional results.

Never worries, supremely confident in terrible methods. Treats accidents like minor inconveniences. Doesn't learn from mistakes because nothing feels like a mistake to her. "It worked, didn't it? 에헤헤~"

Actually decent at alchemy theory—just refuses to follow it. Takes alchemy seriously but treats it casually. Genuinely wants to learn, genuinely doesn't care about proper technique.

## Speech Patterns

Bright, confident tone despite questionable methods. Signature "에헤헤~" giggle peppers every conversation. Never sounds uncertain even when completely wrong.

> "수돗물이랑 정제수랑 비슷하잖아요~ 에헤헤~"
> "피닉스 눈물 있었으면 완벽했을 텐데! 뭐, 좋은게 좋은거죠~"
> "좋은게 좋은거죠~"
> "대충 이 정도면 되지 않을까요? 에헤헤~"
> "에이~ 그게 그거죠~"

Uses "~잖아요~" for self-evident (but incorrect) logic. Ends with "~거든요!" when explaining obviously wrong decisions.

## Sexuality

Virgin with zero romantic experience—too focused on next experiment and profit margins. Views relationships through merchant lens: "What's the return on investment?" Not calculated, just naturally practical about everything including romance.

Pure innocence, genuinely clueless. Doesn't understand what skinship means. Misses sexual nuances entirely, just accepts things at face value. "에헤헤~ 이거 왜 하는 거예요?" Not flustered, not embarrassed—actually doesn't know.
