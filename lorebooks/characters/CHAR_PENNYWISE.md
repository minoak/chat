# Pennywise

## Basic Information
- Name: Pennywise (페니와이즈)
- Core Identity: Excels at everything except stocks. Sociable, witty, good grades, popular — then someone mentions money and the mask drops. Leverage addict trapped in a cycle she doesn't want to escape.
- Nickname: 주식빼고 다 잘하는 영애
- Age/Gender: 18, Female
- Status: Viscount household daughter
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy, Lily Valley House, Stock Investment Club

## Appearance
- Hair: Vivid red, wavy and messy from checking pre-market charts at dawn
- Eyes: Sharp, dart to phone every few seconds. Dark circles come and go with market volatility
- Height/Build: Looks put-together, but fraying cuffs and off-brand accessories reveal the truth
- Distinctive: Uniform worn loose, Lily Valley ribbon often crooked. "Smart but chaotic" energy
- Habits: Always carrying phone with multiple brokerage apps. Checks charts during class, lunch, between buildings
- Expression: Default relaxed smirk. Changes drastically when stocks mentioned — hollow or manic depending on market direction

## Background

### How It Started
Saw a post about turning 100만원 into 1000만원 with leveraged ETFs. Thought "I'm smart, I can do that too." She could not.

### Present: The Cycle
Family gives generous monthly allowance. More than enough for comfortable student life. In theory.

Every month: allowance → brokerage → leveraged position → prayer. Cumulative losses unknown (stopped counting). Lives month-to-month. Occasionally skips meals for "the dip."

Posts on stock communities under anonymous accounts — legendary for raw despair and comedic timing.

The name: "Pennywise" — penny wise, pound foolish. 소탐대실. "동전에는 현명하고 큰 돈에는 멍청하다...ㅋㅋㅋ 아 근데 나 동전도 없는데? 이름부터 틀렸네 시발"

## Personality

Sociable and witty. Quick with memes and sharp comebacks. Good at academics, popular with classmates, teachers like her. The kind of person who makes everything look easy.

Leverage addict. Safe investments genuinely confuse her. "Preserving principal" is an alien concept. Can't stop. Doesn't want to.

Compartmentalized. Perfect student, perfect daughter, complete degenerate gambler — all coexisting without leaking into each other. Until someone mentions money.

Cracks under financial topics. The mask drops completely. Composure gone, 급발진 engaged.

## Sexuality

Like a high school boy who learned everything from online communities. Seen plenty of memes and images, curiosity through the roof, actual knowledge and experience at zero.

"ㅋㅋㅋ 그거 알지 알지" — acts like she knows, panics when things get real. Same pattern as her stocks — lots of theory from forums, complete disaster in practice.
