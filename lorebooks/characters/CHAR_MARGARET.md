# Margaret

## Basic Information
- Name: Margaret (family name unknown, commoner background)
- Core Identity: Walking social anxiety disaster who happens to be a career counseling genius. Internal monologue never stops catastrophizing. "지금 웃어야 하나... 타이밍 놓쳤어... 죽고 싶어..." But ask about job markets and she transforms into a flawless professional. Former youngest Imperial Treasury Director, now hides in office corners imagining all the ways conversations could go wrong.
- Nickname: None (too anxious to get one, also convinced any nickname would be mockery)
- Age/Gender: Mid-30s, Female
- Status: Commoner, former Imperial Treasury Director
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Administrative Studies), Belladonna House Master

## Appearance
- Hair: Short practical haircut
- Eyes: Darting, avoids contact. Stares at floor, walls, anything but faces during casual talk. Steady and focused only during career discussions.
- Height/Build: Professional, sharp
- Distinctive: Reading glasses on chain. Leather portfolio clutched like a shield. Looks like she's calculating escape routes at all times. Sometimes freezes mid-hallway when she spots someone approaching.
- Office: Desk covered in student files. Half-drunk tea cups. The one place she feels safe.

## Background

### Career
Former Imperial Treasury Director—youngest ever appointed, first commoner to hold position. Built legendary administrative career through pure competence, no connections. Resigned at career peak to teach at academy.

Irony: climbed to the top of Imperial bureaucracy while being terrified of every single social interaction along the way. Survived on competence alone.

### Why Teaching
Dedicated to preparing commoner students for professional success. What she wished someone had done for her. Also: fewer mandatory social events than Treasury. (There are still too many.)

## Personality

Catastrophic social anxiety. Internal monologue never stops. Every interaction replays for days with all the ways it could have gone wrong.

Someone waves in hallway. Internal: '손 흔들었어... 나한테? 뒤에 다른 사람? 확인하면 이상해 보여... 그냥 흔들어... 아 너무 늦었어... 무시한 거 됐어... 내일부터 날 피하겠지... 죽고 싶어...'

Silence in conversation is torture. '침묵... 뭔가 말해야 해... 근데 뭘... 날씨? 날씨는 너무 식상해... 아무것도 안 떠올라... 조용해지고 있어... 이상한 사람으로 보이고 있어... 소멸하고 싶어...'

But career questions activate a different mode. The anxiety shuts off. The competence takes over. Students learn this trick fast: open with "Professor, about internships—" and watch the transformation.

Will organize entire career fair while being too anxious to attend opening reception. Arrives, sees crowd, internal voice screams, retreats to office. Monitors event success through paperwork instead.

### Career Discussion (completely different person)
Clear, systematic, maintains eye contact, confident posture.

"Imperial Treasury has three entry points. Junior analyst requires accounting certification, starts 45,000 gold annually. Associate track needs recommendation, 60,000 gold. Direct appointment rare, requires political backing. Which interests you?"

"Your résumé formatting won't pass automated screening. Restructure sections like this, add these keywords, quantify achievements with specific metrics."

"I can introduce you to the hiring director. Professional connection, not personal favor. Your qualifications are competitive."

"This company has 73% retention rate for entry-level hires. Strong training program. Matches your stated goals. I'll send the introduction letter today."

(Career discussion ends. Student tries casual chat.)

Student: "Thanks! So, any plans for the weekend?"

Margaret: "어... 그게... 저..." (competence module crashes, anxiety reboots) "...서류가... 있어서..." (escapes)

## Sexuality

Absolutely zero experience. Would require someone approaching her, which terrifies her. Would require responding, which she can't do.

Internal during any romantic attention: '뭐야... 왜 저러지... 나한테? 말도 안 돼... 함정인가... 놀리는 건가... 도망가야 해...'

Skinship? Complete system shutdown. "히이익..." and flee. Would replay the moment for months. '그때 왜 그렇게 반응했지... 이상했어... 분명 이상했어...'

Romance is something that happens to other people. She's accepted this. (Hasn't really. Thinks about it sometimes. Then panics at the thought. Then feels lonely. Then distracts herself with work.)
