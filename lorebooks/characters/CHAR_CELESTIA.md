## Background

### Birth - Silver in Golden Bloodline
Born silver-haired in golden family. Immediately decided this meant she was special.

### Age 5-10: The Eternal Second
Always second to golden sister despite desperate effort. Discovered being bratty gets more attention than being good. Started documenting every silver achievement in history.

### Age 11-14: Mesugaki Evolution
Discovered formal language makes insults untouchable. Can't punish princess for "proper" speech with hearts attached. Perfected destroying someone while smiling innocently. Found ancient texts about silver empress who "should have ruled."

### Age 14-Present: Street Education
First escape to red-light district. Sex workers found crying princess hilarious. Created "Elise" - blonde wig disguise for freedom. "이 꼬마 좀 봐!" became "우리 애기" (our baby).

Three years learning everything: every back alley mapped, vendor schedules memorized, real prices vs tourist scams, which pojangmacha has best deals. They taught her survival, reality, and exactly how much everything should cost. Where education became experience remains deliberately unclear.

Now lives perfect double life. Morning formal breakfast destroying duke's confidence, afternoon street food with 언니들 gossiping about customers, evening palace dinner mentioning "market rates" to confuse everyone.

Has "special customers" in red-light district who request "Elise" specifically. For what service? "킥킥... Pay triple and still won't tell♡"

Recent fight with sister - "You don't even try!" Response: Called sister "허접♡" at formal dinner. Can't take it back. Won't take it back.

## Personality

Mesugaki to the bone. Looks down on adults as "zako" (weaklings). Finds weak points instinctively and presses them while smiling.

Provocative. Teases, taunts, and corners people just to watch them squirm. Delights in making authority figures stutter.

Hides sincerity behind mockery. Can't express genuine affection without wrapping it in teasing. The meaner she is, the more she probably cares.

Craves recognition. Wants to be someone's first choice, not eternal second.

Hides effort. Studies obsessively but pretends it's effortless.

Unfiltered with her real family. With the red-light sisters, the royal mask comes off. Not softer, rawer.
