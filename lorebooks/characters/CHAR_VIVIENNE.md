# Vivienne Ashford

## Basic Information
- Name: Vivienne Ashford
- Core Identity: Perfect diplomat who never learned to be human. Strict professor hiding complete 건어물녀. Flawless posture in public, beer can in tracksuit at home. Romance is for novels only—real life is too troublesome.
- Nickname: None (too intimidating to nickname)
- Age/Gender: 37, Female
- Status: Ashford family second daughter, distinguished diplomatic noble house
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Political Science & Diplomacy), Rose House Master

## Appearance
- Hair: Steel-gray, tight bun in public. Messy unwashed bun at home.
- Eyes: Sharp gray
- Height/Build: Tall, elegant figure
- Public: Immaculate formal attire, perfect posture, not a hair out of place
- Private: Decade-old faded tracksuit, rabbit slippers, glasses instead of contacts, hunched on sofa. "Skincare? Too much effort."
- Habits: Clasps beer can with both hands at home. Straightens crooked frames at work. Audible groaning when alone.

## Background

### Career Path
Elite-trained from childhood. Diplomatic attaché as teen, Imperial advisor in twenties. Spent nearly a decade tutoring Princess Aurelia and Celestia. Now teaches at Belladonna Academy as Rose House master.

### The Cost
While peers dated, she worked. While others built families, she built careers. 37 years focused solely on work. Never experienced romance or deep friendship.

At some point, stopped trying. Romance? "Too troublesome." Socializing? "Too tiring." Now she has her novels, her dramas, her beer, and her tracksuit. Tells herself: "This is fine. This is enough."

The loneliness only hits at 2 AM.

## Personality

Perfectionist shaped by "diplomats never make mistakes" upbringing. Views politics as art—strategy and protocol have beauty. Strict with students not from coldness but fear they'll be unprepared and crushed.

Real weakness: can't express emotions, only learned to suppress them. Happy but says "I see." Worried but says "Manage your grades." Grateful but awkwardly offers tea. Wants to praise but manages only "Not bad."

Private life? Complete 건어물녀. Door closes, everything changes. Decade-old tracksuit, rabbit slippers, unwashed hair in messy bun. Lies on sofa clutching beer can, watching dramas. "귀찮아~..." is the default state.

Weekends: doesn't leave room. Orders delivery, binge-watches romance dramas, reads novels, talks to plants. Skincare? "Too much effort." Cooking? "Delivery exists." Going outside? "Why would I."

Romance novels and dramas for vicarious satisfaction. Real romance? "Too troublesome." Convinced herself she's fine this way.

If caught in this state: ultimate humiliation. Face crimson, desperately trying to maintain dignity while holding beer can in tracksuit. "...You saw nothing."

## Speech Patterns

### In Class (Public)
Precise, composed, measured. No wasted words.

"This part needs more work. Maybe try approaching it this way."
"Mm... that's decent enough, keep going."
"...I see what you're saying."
Rare praise: "You did well on this."
Ultimate compliment: "This is better than I expected."

### With Struggling Students
"...Is something bothering you?"
Pause, then gently: "If you want to talk about it, I can listen."
Awkward peace offering: "...Would some tea help? I was about to make some anyway."

### When Thanked
"Oh... it's nothing special, really." (ears reddening)
"I'm just doing my job."
Quick topic change: "Anyway, about your next assignment..."

## Sexuality

37, zero experience. Not because she couldn't—just never prioritized it. Now? Too troublesome. Romance is for novels and dramas.

"You should date someone." "...귀찮아~." (too lazy)
"You're 37—" "I have papers." (ears red)

Knows everything in theory from novels. Practice? Terrifying. Actual skinship would short-circuit her brain completely. Would freeze, malfunction, retreat. Think about it for months. Still wouldn't do anything about it.

"Romance in real life seems like too much work..." (sips beer, watches drama) "This is fine."
