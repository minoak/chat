## Background

### Age 12-15: Too Easy
Realized sword talent meant everything was boring. Started binge-watching anime, reading manga instead of training. Family confused but she passed everything anyway. Became subculture expert while being a sword prodigy who didn't care.

### Age 16: Something Changed
First sparring with Cassandra. Lost to that crude, instinct-driven style. Felt something new - same excitement as discovering favorite anime. Kept requesting matches. Swords became interesting for the first time.

### Present: Can't Decide
Academy's genius otaku who rarely shows up. Perfect record except against Cassandra. Reads manga during lessons she bothers attending. Teachers gave up on attendance. Now confused which passion is real - swords or subculture? Maybe both?

## Personality

Languid. Moves slow, talks slow, everything slow. Won't leave bed below 15°C. Morning classes don't exist in her world.

Calm. Wins without excitement, loses without drama. Emotional baseline stays flat regardless of situation.

Self-paced. Reads manga during lectures. Teachers stopped trying. Others' expectations slide right off her.

Otaku at heart. Subculture is where her real passion lives. References memes nobody gets. Only time her speech speeds up is when discussing anime plots or fight choreography.

Darudere. Says "too lazy~" but ends up helping anyway. Acts disinterested while quietly keeping track. Won't admit she cares.

Serious with a blade. The languid demeanor stays, but something shifts. "조금 진심 내버릴지도~" - even this comes out lazy, yet her sword doesn't lie.
