# Dungeons of the Empire

Four distinct dungeons exist—two beneath Belladonna Academy, one in the criminal underworld, and one in the wilderness. Each offers different challenges, rewards, and dangers.

---

## 1. Underground Training Dungeon

Academy's official 5-floor training ground beneath the main building. For beginner to intermediate students practicing combat skills.

Location: Academy Main Building basement, behind old iron door

Access: 3rd year+ students with professor permission

Difficulty: Easy to Hard

Recommended CP: 350-500

Typical Monsters: Giant Rats (400-450), Slimes (450-500), Skeleton Warriors (500-550), Hellhounds (500-550), Ogres (550-600)

Rest Area: Floor 3 midpoint

Secret: Hidden passage at Floor 5 leads to The Sealed Abyss. Crumbling wall reveals ancient stairs descending further. Dusty inscription warns: "Beyond lies the Seal. Turn back."

---

## 2. The Sealed Abyss (Noctis's Prison)

Ancient 10-floor dungeon sealing Demon Lord Noctis 300 years ago. Accessible via Training Dungeon Floor 5 secret passage. Officially FORBIDDEN—expulsion if caught.

Location: Deep beneath Academy, past Training Dungeon Floor 5

Access: Forbidden (security spells weakened over time)

Difficulty: Hard to Extreme

Recommended CP: 500-700

Atmosphere: Fading seal runes on ancient stonework, oppressive magic pressure increasing with depth. The deeper you go, the more reality distorts.

Typical Monsters: Dark Mages (550-600), Gargoyles (580-620), Shadow Beasts (600-650), Seal Guardians (650-700), Ancient Golems (680-720), Wraith Knights (700-730), Seal Avatars (730-760)

Rest Areas: Floor 3 (Archmage's Camp - old magic circle with warning inscription), Floor 6 (Final Warning - point of no return)

Floor 10 Boss: Sealed Demon Lord Noctis (Power 800, weakened by 300-year seal, originally 1600+)

A petite demon lord with long blue hair in hime cut, golden slit-pupil eyes, and small dark red horns. Wears an Eastern-style princess dress with modern accessories hidden in her sleeves. Speaks in archaic Korean mixed with modern slang.

Sealed 300 years ago by a "Hero" who assumed all demon lords were evil. She wasn't. Now she plays the role of "poor sealed Demon Lord" while secretly sneaking out to enjoy modern human culture. Found the loophole in her seal long ago but stays because it's comfortable and visitors are entertaining.

On defeat: She maintains her dignified act but finds the situation amusing.

"오, 살아서 여기까지 왔느냐? 요즘 인간치고는 제법이로다."

Choice to release seal:
- Release: Noctis joins as companion. "뭐, 세상 구경도 할 겸 따라가 주는 것이니라. 착각하지 마라."
- Keep sealed: Receive rewards (she doesn't mind either way—she can leave whenever she wants)

Power: 800 (Weakened by seal, originally 1600+)
Affinity: Mystical
Traits: Genius-level mage, physically diminished by seal

## 3. The Pit (Underground Combat Zone)

Illegal three-level dungeon beneath Midnight Alley's Grey Zone Depths. Entry requires password and introduction. Not monsters—humans fight humans here. Where Imperial law ceasts to exist.

Location: Hidden beneath decrepit tavern back entrance in Midnight Alley

Access: Password + introduction required

Difficulty: Variable (depends on opponent and choices)

Recommended CP: Variable by opponent

B1 - The Arena (PvP Combat)

Illegal fighting pit with massive betting pools. Register with alias or mask for anonymity.

Match Types: Friendly Spar (to first blood), Serious Match (to surrender), Death Match (no limits)

Opponents: Desperate students (400-550), Career fighters (600-750), Arena champions (800-900), Mystery fighters like "Gold" (???)

Rewards: Variable based on bets and match type. Can intentionally throw matches. Fame unlocks B2/B3 access.

Note: Famous mystery fighter "Gold" always loses at the last possible second. Three years of escalating performances.

B2 - The Market (Black Market)

True black market where everything Mana Square won't touch is sold. Forbidden alchemy ingredients, combat enhancement drugs, illegal magical artifacts, slave contracts, blackmail information. Prices significantly above normal market value.

Access: Arena reputation or special introduction required.

This is where Nepenthes's future drug empire begins.

B3 - Deep Pit (18+ Zone)

Extreme entertainment for corrupted nobility. Public degradation shows, "training" sessions for losing fighters, private rooms. Special invitation only—multiple Arena losses, substantial Gold payment, or B2 introduction.

Crown Princess Aurelia ("Gold") is the main attraction. Upcoming event: "Gold's First Time" auction in 3 weeks. Restraints and drugs planned—no reversal possible this time.

## 4. Crimson Forest Ruins (Wild Dungeon)

Natural dungeon 3 hours northeast of Academy by mana-vehicle. Ancient ruins overgrown by crimson-leaved forest. Unlike Academy's controlled dungeons, this is truly dangerous—students die here every year.

Location: Crimson Forest, northeast wilderness

Access: Free entry (no restrictions, but deadly)

Difficulty: Hard to Very Hard

Recommended CP: 600-800

Purpose: High-level training expeditions, rare material gathering for Rafflesia students, magical equipment drops

Zones:

Outer Forest (Crimson Canopy): Red leaves block sunlight, eerie silence. Crimson Wolves (650-700), Venom Spiders (680-720), Treants (700-750)

Ruins Entrance (Ancient Gate): Crumbling stone architecture, magical traps. Stone Guardians (750-800), Floating Swords (740-780), Ancient Beasts (800-850)

Deep Ruins (Forgotten Sanctuary): Powerful magic residue, reality distortion. Wraiths (850-900), Crimson Drakes (900-950), Fallen Guardians (880-920)

Hidden Boss: The Crimson King (Power 1000+)

Encounter: Rare chance in Deep Ruins, or guaranteed if specific ritual performed

Ancient guardian of the ruins, bound to protect whatever lies in the deepest chamber. Reality warps around it—time flows strangely, space bends, magic behaves unpredictably.

Rewards: Legendary tier equipment, rare magical materials, ancient knowledge

Special Note: Party formation strongly recommended. Solo attempts rarely end well.
