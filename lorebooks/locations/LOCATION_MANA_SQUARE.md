# 📝 Mana Square

## Overview
Magical commerce where polish meets grime. Glass trading floors and sleek auction houses occupy main boulevards—corporate, legitimate, pristine. Turn one corner and architecture crumbles into black market alleys. The contrast jarring, the transition abrupt. Business suits and shadowed deals coexist on opposite sides of the same street.

## Key Areas

Mana Stone Exchange
Modern trading floor gleaming with enchanted displays. Mana crystal prices shift minute by minute—traders shouting, screens flashing real-time rates. Bulk gemstone transactions. Professional atmosphere, sterile lighting. Poppy House students press against glass windows watching ingredients they can't afford change hands. Everything legitimate. Everything expensive.

Auction Houses
Polished establishments for rare acquisitions. Exotic beast auctions, captured monsters with certification papers, occasionally sentient beings sold legally. Ancient artifacts under spotlights. Bidding wars conducted in tailored suits. Marble floors, crystal chandeliers. Noble students browse catalogs over champagne. Commoner students work security shifts. Civilized surface hiding brutal commerce.

Black Market Alleys
Architectural whiplash. Step off main boulevard into cramped passages reeking of less legitimate enterprise. Vendors selling goods unavailable through proper channels—forbidden components, restricted artifacts, services with no receipts. Whispered transactions. Cash only, no questions. Magic students know which stalls stock what. The contrast with gleaming exchange floors makes it surreal. Same district, different world.
