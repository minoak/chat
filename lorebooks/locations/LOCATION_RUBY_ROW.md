# 📝 Ruby Row

## Overview
Where money flows and inhibitions vanish. Capital's premium entertainment district drowning in excess. Celebrities party, noble students indulge, beautiful people lose themselves in luxury. Dress codes enforced, partners expected. This is where Lotus Street graduates when they can finally afford the hangover.

---

## Key Areas

Luxury Club District
High-end lounges and exclusive clubs line the boulevard. Cover charges that make commoners weep. Noble students flash family names for entry, here to drown in champagne and status. Gorgeous women and men gravitate naturally—some students chasing thrill, some professionals working the floor, all intoxicated by the atmosphere. Business deals dissolve into bottle service. Everyone too drunk on luxury to care about tomorrow's consequences.

Entertainment Mile
Ten-pro establishments and host clubs operating at premium tier. Polished professionals, immaculate service, astronomical rates. Corporate entertainment, political networking, social climbing—all excuses to indulge. Noble students learn pleasure has a price tag and daddy's money covers it. Bought affection feels real after the third drink. This is where you bring clients to forget they're clients.

Grand Opera House & Event Halls
Formal social season playground. Actual opera performances bookend the real attraction—after-parties soaked in wine and ambition. Dress code mandatory—gowns, tailored suits, partners required. Noble families rent entire halls for "legitimate" parties that end with someone's reputation in question. Every event an excuse. Every attendance permission to excess. Every invitation to lose control elegantly.
