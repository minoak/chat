# 📝 Scarlet Street 

## Overview
Main college town strip directly outside academy gates. Heart of student life where licensed club activities mix with grey-market hustles. Illegal hologram ads shriek through crowds while student-run stalls hawk everything from sanctioned snacks to dubious potions. Commoner housing sprawls behind the commercial chaos.

## Key Areas

Boulevard & Shopping District
Official student life on display. Club-approved food stalls and magic item pop-ups line the main drag, all vetted by student council. Malls and brand shops accept mana crystal cards alongside regular payment. Aesthetic cafés overflow with students nursing assignments over overpriced lattes. Diagon Alley-style shops sell prank items, certified oddities, and magical ingredients. Student-made illegal hologram ads screech through the streets, phasing through pedestrians, driving everyone insane. Nobles browse designer sections while commoners stick to budget racks.

After-Class Bazaar 
Grey zone that materializes daily when classes end. Alchemy students dump defective potions at clearance prices—half-off stamina draughts that might cause temporary blindness. Magic department hawks failed enchantments. Belladonna students spread tarps for secondhand markets—clothes, accessories, random junk. Technically illegal but enforcement is a joke. Occasional crackdown, then back to business.

Residential Zone
Studio apartments and cramped rooms packed behind the commercial strip. Commoner students who can't or won't use dorms cram into housing ranging from livable to depressing, prices wildly variable. 24-hour convenience stores on every corner sell instant ramen and energy drinks. Late-night taverns, PC cafés, pool halls provide escape until dawn. Nobles never venture this deep—they commute from upscale districts in mana-vehicles.
