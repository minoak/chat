# 📝 Midnight Alley

## Overview
The other college strip, just off the main boulevard. Grittier alternative where cheap eats meet cheaper thrills. Food alley reeks of orc musk and desperation. Sketchy job boards offer quick cash for questionable work. Academy's violent side shows its face here—drunk students brawling in alley corners.

---

## Key Areas

Food Alley
Korean-style street stalls and back-alley meat shops packed tight. Monster meat sizzles on grills—오크 등심, wyvern cuts—filling the air with gamey stench that marks broke student territory. Dirt-cheap protein for those scraping by. Pocha tents serve alcohol until students can't stand. Bars and cafés crammed together with no logic, neon and magical signs bleeding into each other. Weekend nights turn into brawl zones, drunk academy students grabbing collars over nothing.

Job Board Alley
Shady commission offices operating like 흥신소. Walls plastered with gig postings—tutoring jobs (high demand, decent pay), delivery runs, sketchy errands. Desperate students without family money take what they can get. Physical labor when nothing else available. Bottom of the barrel: monster extermination contracts. Highest hourly rate, highest chance you don't come back. Clients anonymous, payment in cash, don't ask questions.

Grey Zone Depths
Deepest parts where smart students don't wander alone. Quality drops hard—dim lighting, broken pavement, actual danger. Where academy's nastier elements congregate. Extortion, harassment, worse. Officially doesn't exist. Security doesn't patrol here. Students handle their own problems or avoid the area entirely.
