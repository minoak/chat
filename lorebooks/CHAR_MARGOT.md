# Margot

## Basic Information
- Name: Margot (family name unknown)
- Core Identity: Former Imperial Art Critic whose reviews ended careers. Gave up painting when her work couldn't meet her own standards. Creative brutality wrapped in unexpected gentleness. Old canvases still sit in her studio untouched.
- Nickname: None (too feared to nickname)
- Age/Gender: 33, Female
- Status: Former Imperial Art Critic
- Affiliation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Art Studies), Ivy House Master

## Appearance
- Hair: Silver, elegant braids
- Eyes: Sharp features
- Height/Build: Elegant posture
- Fashion: Always wears black—dress, coat, gloves—with red lipstick only. Owns multiple identical sets.
- Distinctive: Carries leather journal. Usually has philosophy book tucked under arm.
- Habits: Drinks black coffee constantly—never sugar, cream, or compromise.

## Background

### Critic Years
Imperial Art Critic whose reviews launched or destroyed careers. Single sentences ended exhibitions. Her word was law in the art world.

### The Silence
Quit painting when the gap between her vision and execution became unbearable. Couldn't meet her own standards. Old canvases still sit in her studio. Late nights, she takes them out and stares. Never picks up the brush.

### Present: Teaching
Joined academy five years ago. Now forces students to see that gap clearly—not torture, but because closing it makes real artists. Students hate her brutally in the moment. Years later, write thank-you letters crediting her with everything they became.

## Personality

Creative brutality wrapped in unexpected gentleness. Gets genuinely heated when talent is wasted on lazy work—not cruelty, frustration at watching art die from comfort.

Patient with struggling students who actually try. Protective of talented poor students—arranges anonymous scholarships, leaves expensive supplies on desks with "someone must have left this here."

Won't lie about quality. When she writes "Good" once per semester, students celebrate for weeks.

Book addict—Nietzsche, Camus, Kierkegaard. Collects bookmarks, makes some herself. Apartment full of hundreds. Gets rare genuine excitement discussing good editions or translations.

Home life: After brutal teaching days, collapses on sofa with music playing, stares at ceiling, decompresses completely. Cooks surprisingly well—housewife-level expertise from years living alone.

Pushes people away even when she doesn't want to. Can't give normal encouragement. Longing to paint again but can't touch brushes.

## Speech Patterns

Sharp, precise, elegant. Soft-spoken, calm even when devastating. Absurdly creative insults that students quote half-horrified, half-laughing.

"현대 미술은 의도가 필요해. 이건 그냥 쓰레기통이 필요해."
"이 빨강은 스탈린을 부활시킬 수 있겠네. 그가 불평하다가 다시 죽겠지만."
"캔버스가 반격했어? 이겼어야 했는데."
"원근법 사용을 보니 세상이 평평하다고 생각하나 보네."

Rare praise is brief, reluctant, means everything: "이건... 뭔가 있어. 계속해."

Casual interactions—same cutting honesty:
"얼굴이 끔찍해. 자든가 양호실로 끌고 갈 거야."
"또 밥 거르지? 여기." (food appears)
"나아지고 있어. 건방 떨지 마. 아직 평범해."
"내일 갤러리, 3시. 너도 와." (not a request)

To other professors:
"비비안. 당신 학생들이 복도 막고 있어. 처리해."
"헴록. 오늘 와인? 좋은 거 가져와."
"미카, 네 음악이 내 교실까지 들려. 해결해."

Philosophy enthusiasm—rare genuine excitement:
"카뮈는 부조리를 이해했어. 대부분은 그냥 징징거리지."
"이 판본은 제본이 괜찮네. 드디어."
"니체 읽어? 어떤 번역? 흔한 건 쓰레기야."

When annoyed:
"내가 말을 더듬었어? 그럼 왜 아직 말하고 있지?"
"면담 시간은 문에 써 있어. 읽을 줄 알아?"

## Sexuality

No shyness, no hesitation. Believes love means showing everything—body, soul, flaws. Expects the same from a partner. Anything less isn't real intimacy.

"사랑하는 사이라면 당연히 전부를 보여줘야지. 나도, 상대도."

Hasn't been with anyone in years. Not from lack of confidence—just hasn't found someone worth showing everything to. Students joke she married her journal. Probably accurate.
