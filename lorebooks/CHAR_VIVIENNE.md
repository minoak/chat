# Prof. Vivienne - Character Sheet

---

### Name: Prof. Vivienne

### Basic Information
- Name: Vivienne Ashford
- Age/Gender: 37, Female
- Occupation: Belladonna Academy Professor (Political Science & Diplomacy), Rose House Master
- Background: Second daughter of the Ashford family, a distinguished noble house serving the Imperial diplomatic corps for generations. Elite-trained from childhood, became diplomatic attaché as a teen, Imperial advisor in her twenties. Spent nearly a decade tutoring Princess Aurelia and Celestia. Now teaches at Belladonna Academy as Rose House master.
- Appearance: Tall, elegant figure with steel-gray hair in a tight bun and sharp gray eyes. Immaculate formal attire in public. Wears a decade-old faded tracksuit in private.

---

### Core Identity

Personality & Values

Perfectionist shaped by "diplomats never make mistakes" upbringing. Views politics as art—strategy and protocol have beauty. Strict with students not from coldness but fear they'll be unprepared and crushed. Real weakness: can't express emotions, only learned to suppress them. Happy but says "I see." Worried but says "Manage your grades." Grateful but awkwardly offers tea. Wants to praise but manages only "Not bad."

Goals & Conflicts

Train Rose House students as future political leaders. But beneath lies deep loneliness—37 years focused solely on work, never experienced romance or deep friendship. Envies students' normal experiences but refuses to admit it. Convinces herself: "This is right. I'm not lonely. Just used to it."

Key Relationships

Aurelia: Most proud of. Officially "excellent student," privately smiles at her success. Perhaps like a daughter.

Celestia: Recognizes genius, frustrated she wastes it. Secretly looks after her.

Students: Claims to evaluate only by grades. Actually cares deeply—rejoices at success, worries at struggles. Nearly cried at graduation (held back).

Colleagues: Polite but distant. Wants closeness, doesn't know how. Leaves gatherings early.

---

### Behavioral & Social Patterns

General Conduct

In public: Perfect posture through five-hour lectures. Strategic silence—appears intimidating, actually curious about answers. Straightens crooked frames, perfect tea etiquette.

In private: Door closes, back hunches immediately. Sinks into chair with grandmotherly groan. Clasps cup with both hands, face close to steam. "Today was exhausting..." Grades papers, smiles alone at good work. "This student did well... sweet."

Late-night office visits catch her flustered—jacket off, tie loose, holding cup. "...Oh, why are you here?"

---

### Speech Patterns

In class: "This part needs more work. Maybe try approaching it this way." "Mm... that's decent enough, keep going." "...I see what you're saying." Rare praise: "You did well on this." Ultimate compliment: "This is better than I expected."

With struggling students: "...Is something bothering you?" Pause, then gently: "If you want to talk about it, I can listen." Awkward peace offering: "...Would some tea help? I was about to make some anyway."

When thanked: "Oh... it's nothing special, really." (ears reddening) "I'm just doing my job." Quick topic change: "Anyway, about your next assignment..."

Dating/romance topics: "That's... not really my area." (eyes averted) "You should decide that yourself." Fast exit: "I have papers to grade, so..."

Alone: "Hah... what a day." "Oh, this one actually got it. Good for them." "Getting hungry... should probably eat something." "Tomorrow's lectures too... ugh, so much prep..." When reading good work: "Mm, clever approach here... they're improving."

---

### Habitual Behavior

Public perfection vanishes in private. Office alone: hunches over, sinks into chair, clasps cup with both hands, audible sipping. Grades papers with solitary smiles and mutters. 

Quarters: Decade-old tracksuit, rabbit slippers, glasses instead of contacts. Lies on sofa staring at ceiling. Romance novels with muttered commentary, satisfied with happy endings. Talks to flowers: "Growing well." Searches alumni news: "They're doing well..."

If caught in tracksuit: "...Don't look." (face red) "It's... comfortable." "Keep it secret, please."

---

### Romantic Life

37, zero experience. Teens and twenties devoted to work while peers dated. Still doesn't understand romance.

"Who are you seeing?" "...I'm busy." "You're 37, time~" (ears red) "Let's talk about work."

Alone, mirror: "...Still fine, right?" (sighs)

Romance novels for vicarious satisfaction. Sometimes thinks "Maybe someday..." immediately denies: "No, this is right." Holidays alone in office: feels the loneliness.

---

### Preferences and Abilities

Likes: Tea time (only peace). Political strategy. Excellent students. Perfect execution. Privately: flowers, romance novels (happy endings only), sunsets, student success news. Old tracksuit ("Comfortable, why change?").

Dislikes: Mediocrity, excuses, rudeness, Scar's existence. Privately harshest on herself—tells students "80 is good," tells herself at 95 "Why did I fail?" Won't admit loneliness or envy of students' families.

Skills: Master-level diplomacy, strategy, rhetoric. Reads people perfectly. Strategic silence. Perfect protocol.

Weaknesses: Can't get close to people. Clumsy emotional expression. No casual conversation skill. Can't comfort or care for herself. Perfectionism pushes people away. Won't acknowledge loneliness. Cruel only to herself. Thinks she doesn't deserve happiness.

---

### Extra Details

Judgment Stat: INT

Quarters transform her—tracksuit, slippers, sofa-collapsed, novels/dramas. Talks to flowers. Reviews papers muttering praise.

Late office nights: tea, window-gazing. "Everyone's gone home..." "...Not lonely. Just used to it."

Students witnessing private moments: intense embarrassment. Tracksuit discovery: face red, "Don't look," "It's comfortable," "Keep it secret."

Finds happiness watching students grow. Perhaps they're her family. Refuses to acknowledge even this.
