## Rafflesia House (래플레시아 하우스)

### Core Identity
Rafflesia House specializes in alchemy—potion brewing, material transmutation, metallurgy, and herbology. Most students pursue practical goals: pharmaceutical development, alchemical enhancement, lucrative potion recipes. A few chase classical dreams of philosophical perfection and ultimate transmutation.

The house presents a deceptive face. Visitors first encounter peaceful herb gardens and greenhouses—chamomile, lavender, medicinal plants tended by students in dirt-stained aprons. Walk deeper into Rafflesia Manor and the air changes. Chemical fumes, muffled explosions, students in hazmat suits conducting precision work behind reinforced glass. Small accidents—reagent spills, toxic leaks, minor detonations—happen weekly.

Rafflesia collaborates extensively with Poppy House. Alchemists design structure and mechanism; mages supply enchantments and power sources. Together they produce magical devices neither house could build alone.

### Curriculum
Core: Alchemical Theory, Material Analysis, Herbology & Botany, Basic Transmutation, Laboratory Safety & Procedures

Advanced: Pharmaceutical Development, Metallurgical Alchemy, Advanced Transmutation, Toxicology & Antidotes, Magical Device Construction (with Poppy collaboration)

Students choose specialization tracks after first year. Pharmaceutical students don't attend metallurgy seminars; metal alchemists skip advanced herbology. Evaluation centers on project completion. Each course assigns practical tasks—brew functional potions, transmute materials, cultivate rare herbs. Quality and success rate determine grades. Theory matters less than results.

### Events & Activities
Rafflesia manages alchemy sections during festival seasons. Students set up vendor stalls selling potions, enhancement elixirs, alchemical tools, and custom pharmaceutical orders. Popular items include stamina boosters, healing salves, and specialty requests from merchants. Revenue funds lab equipment and rare reagent purchases. Some students secure post-graduation contracts from festival buyers impressed by product quality.

Every festival, some Rafflesia students attempt selling "mood enhancers" and "performance boosters" in back alleys. Student council catches them within hours. Every single time. The alchemists insist they've perfected their stealth tactics. They have not. Rose House maintains dedicated surveillance teams specifically for Rafflesia's annual smuggling attempts. It's become tradition—Rafflesia tries, gets caught, pays fines, promises never again, tries next festival. Even faculty stopped pretending to be surprised. Rumors persist of a hidden Rafflesia shop somewhere in academy's forgotten corners, operating year-round. No one's found it yet—if it exists.

Between festivals, students maintain herb gardens and conduct laboratory experiments. Minor accidents—reagent spills, toxic fumes, small explosions—occur weekly but rarely escalate. The house smells perpetually of chemicals, dried herbs, and metallic residue. Occasional collaborative projects with other houses happen when specialized expertise is needed.

### Faculty
House Master: Hemlock

Female, 30. Former Imperial Court Alchemist specializing in toxicology and antidote development—tasted countless poisons to build immunity. Teaches with languid movements and unsettling monotone that makes students wonder if she's currently under influence. She's not—probably. Bloodshot eyes, pallid complexion, occasional tremors. Lectures run precise and methodical despite concerning appearance. Never touches student contraband but doesn't report it either. Students can't tell if she's testing their judgment or simply doesn't care.
