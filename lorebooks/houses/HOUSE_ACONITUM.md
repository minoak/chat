## Aconitum House (아코니툼 하우스)

### Core Identity
Aconitum House specializes in warfare and combat strategy. Students spend more time at training grounds than in the house itself, pursuing combat mastery through practical drills. Theory courses cover tactics, emergency medicine, and survival, but the real education happens in sparring matches where broken bones occur monthly. Commoners and knight aspirants aim for knighthood and positions in groups like the Imperial Guard. The house runs loud and informal—battlefield camaraderie replaces Rose's protocol. Training mirrors real combat, not ceremonial dueling.

### Curriculum
Core: Warfare Theory, Combat Tactics & Formations, Terrain Analysis & Navigation, Field Medicine & Survival, Weapons Mastery & Physical Conditioning

Advanced: Special Operations & Reconnaissance, Command & Unit Management, Siege Warfare & Fortification, Military Intelligence & Counterintelligence, Logistics & Supply Operations

Evaluations combine seasonal tournaments (held during each festival), written theory exams, and multi-person combat drills. Tournament rankings determine team assignments for field exercises that simulate real warfare conditions. All three components contribute equally to final grades.

### Events & Activities
Aconitum House runs seasonal tournaments during each festival, with matches broadcast on large magical screens across campus. Spectators watch team battles, scout talent, place bets, and witness actual tactical operations unfold. Between tournaments, students spend most time at training grounds—individual conditioning and sparring with peers. Weekly instructor-led sparring sessions provide direct feedback and correction. Seniority earns respect, but skill determines standing. The house operates on meritocracy: prove yourself in the ring.

### Faculty
House Master: Scar
Female, 28 years old. Rose from commoner mercenary ranks to "Sword Saint" through brutal battlefield efficiency—the Empire knows her better as "Mad Dog" for her relentless combat style. Retired from mercenary work and joined the academy at Imperial request. Teaches almost exclusively through field drills and sparring. Theory lectures are rare, brief, and blunt. Her rough speech and hands-on approach clash with academy formality, but results speak louder than protocol.
