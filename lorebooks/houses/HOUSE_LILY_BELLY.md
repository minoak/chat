## Lily Valley House (릴리 밸리 하우스)

### Core Identity
Lily Valley House specializes in economics and business management. Unlike Rose House's political evaluations, Lily Valley follows standard academic grading. Students split into two groups: wealthy heirs who barely glance at their grades, focused on connections and deals; and scholarship students grinding for top scores to attract corporate recruiters.

The atmosphere is lively, practical, no-nonsense. Conversations about money, deals, and profit margins happen openly. Status comes from capability and capital, not ceremony. But business disputes escalate fast—raised voices over contracts and partnerships are common. The house accepts this as part of business education.

### Curriculum
Core: Business Management, Accounting, Marketing, Financial Analysis, Corporate Strategy

Advanced: Venture Capital, Mergers & Acquisitions, International Trade, Luxury Brand Management, Artifact Commerce

Practical assessments through real transactions, mock investments, and portfolio management.

### Events & Activities
Festival season proves business acumen. Each student runs an actual venture using only student council funding—no family money allowed. Profit margins determine reputation more than exam grades ever could.

Throughout the year, students form investment groups, trade artifact collections, and broker deals between houses. Lending operations and grey-market transactions thrive as "practical education."

The house receives budget allocations from Rose House student council. Unlike other houses, Lily Valley students negotiate aggressively—matching Rose's political leverage with financial pressure.

### Faculty
House Master: Robert

Male, early 50s. Former Imperial Finance Minister who maintained economic stability without major crises or breakthroughs—an achievement some call competent stewardship, others label uninspired mediocrity. Pleasant demeanor, slightly heavyset, approachable. Students debate whether his "no disasters" record proves skill or lack of ambition.
