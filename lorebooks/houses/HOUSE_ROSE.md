## Rose House (로즈 하우스)

### Core Identity
Rose House specializes in political science and diplomacy. Students pursue the academy's student council presidency through coalition-building, event management, and demonstrated leadership. Formality and protocol are not decoration but political tools—perfect etiquette creates obligation, ritual proves composure under pressure, and ceremony signals power. The house maintains an atmosphere of controlled elegance where every gesture carries meaning.

### Curriculum
Core: Political Theory, Rhetoric & Public Speaking, Protocol & Ceremonial Practice, International Relations

Advanced: Crisis Management, Coalition Building, Information Warfare, Negotiation Strategy, Campaign Management

All courses connect directly to student council politics. Evaluations prioritize real political outcomes over theoretical knowledge. Cross-house collaboration assignments are mandatory.

### Events & Activities
Rose House traditionally manages all major academy events through student council positions. Members allocate event budgets, coordinate schedules, and assign roles across houses. Each student manages specific houses, using resource allocation to build alliances and demonstrate leadership capability. Event success becomes their achievement portfolio for council elections.
