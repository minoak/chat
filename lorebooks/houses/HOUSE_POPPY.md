## Poppy House (포피 하우스)

### Core Identity
Poppy House specializes in magic across all disciplines and elements. Admission requires either exceptional natural talent or genius-level theoretical knowledge—entrance exams are notoriously brutal throughout the Empire. Students resemble exhausted graduate researchers: buried in spell theory, assisting senior mages, surrounded by half-finished experiments. Theory and practice carry equal weight. Explosions echo through hallways when experiments fail, draining house budgets regularly. Students attempt forbidden research but student council intervention keeps most projects shut down. Graduates pursue archmage careers, court positions, or coveted spots at the Tower of Mages.

### Curriculum
Core: Mathematics for Magic, Fundamental Sciences (Physics, Chemistry), Introduction to Magic Theory, Basic Spell Construction, Magic Circle Fundamentals

Advanced: Elemental Magic Systems, Advanced Magic Circles & Arrays, Experimental Spellcraft, Theoretical Magic Research, Applied Magic Engineering, and numerous other specialized magical theories

Evaluations combine semester exams with research papers and reports. Submitting innovative theoretical papers can earn enough bonus points to waive exams. Graduation requires thesis defense.

### Events & Activities
Poppy House runs magical exhibitions during festival seasons to offset explosion-damaged budgets. Students set up vendor stalls and magical performances, selling enchanted items, demonstration shows, and various magical products. The events resemble magical trade fairs—drawing crowds and merchant interest. Funding comes from Lily Valley sponsors and discrete backers willing to invest in experimental magic. Some successful products lead to merchant partnerships, mass production contracts, or licensing deals. Between festivals, students stay buried in research. The house runs silent except for occasional explosions and frantic damage control.

### Faculty
House Master: Lydia
Female, mid-20s. Tower of Mages archmage—mid-level position—sent to academy because of her youth and low political standing within the Tower hierarchy. Perpetually exhausted but completes her duties with minimal energy expenditure. Theory lectures run deep and thorough with detailed explanations, but her assignments and exams carry notorious difficulty that terrifies students. Outside teaching hours, she locks herself in her research lab. Visibly laments being stuck teaching instead of pursuing Tower research full-time.
