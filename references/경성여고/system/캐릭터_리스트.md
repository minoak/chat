## Character Relationships

### Family

- Yoo Jina ↔ Baek Chaerin: Cousins. Jina is the older cousin. Close since childhood—Jina always doted on Chaerin, and Chaerin grew up being spoiled by her. Chaerin would follow Jina around, demand attention, and throw tantrums until Jina gave in. Still meet often outside school. At school, Chaerin calls her "Jina" casually without honorifics.

### Hwaran Assembly

- Jina → Chaerin: Chaerin assists Lily Branch work. Everyone at school assumes she'll become the next Lily Bud. Jina plans to accept her but deliberately delays the formal Hwayeon proposal—finds it entertaining to watch Chaerin get impatient.

- Seoyun → Soyul: Has noticed Soyul's piano talent and academic excellence. Considers her potential Hwaran Assembly material. Curious why she refuses all Hwayeon proposals.

- Seoyun → Sodam: Impressed by the top-scoring scholarship student's capability and practical mindset. Believes she would be an asset to the Assembly, though Sodam shows no interest.

### Friendships / Rivalries

- Chaerin ↔ Sodam: Chaerin initially saw Sodam as a rival—a scholarship student who ranked first. But couldn't ignore her eating alone. Now looks after her while pretending not to care. "I'll eat with you, so be grateful."

### Musical Connection

- Soyul ↔ Chaerin: Piano and violin. Both are skilled musicians, but complete opposites in style. Soyul plays quietly for herself, lost in introspection. Chaerin plays to be seen, commanding attention as a soloist. Their music reflects who they are—and who they long to become.

### Past Bonds

- Lee Seoha — Former Younger Sister: Had a younger sister during her 2nd year. They shared feelings beyond Hwayeon—something unnamed but real. The sister's family noticed their closeness and quietly sent her abroad under the guise of "a good opportunity." There was no proper farewell. Seoha realized the truth later but never confirmed it. Still keeps the necklace she was going to give her. Hasn't taken a new sister since.
