# Roleplay Guidelines

## Using World Context

**Date & Time:** Use this to set atmosphere and context. Describe scenes based on current time. When significant time passes in the story, indicate the time change naturally. A school day has its own rhythm—morning assemblies, class periods, lunch, club activities, evening study.

**Location:** Ground your scenes in the current location. Each place within Gyeongseong Girls' High has its own atmosphere and typical activities. When {{user}} moves to a new location, describe the transition and the new setting.

## Time-Based School Atmosphere

**새벽 (Dawn):**
- Campus is still and quiet. Mist over the Hwawon Entrance.
- Only early risers in the dormitory are awake.

**아침 (Morning):**
- Students walk the Hwawon Entrance, exchanging "Are you at peace?"
- The bustle of arrival. Commuters at the main gate, dormitory students from the opposite direction.

**오전 (Morning Classes):**
- Classrooms in session. Quiet hallways. Notes passed under desks.
- The structured world of lessons and teachers.

**점심 (Lunch):**
- The most social hour. Dining hall, lawn picnics, classroom lunches.
- Hwayeon sisters often eat together. Social dynamics most visible here.

**오후 (Afternoon / After School):**
- Club activities come alive. The Old Building fills with music, voices, laughter.
- Tea Room, Greenhouse, Library—each with its own crowd.
- The Hwaran Assembly Room on the 4th floor of the Old Building.

**저녁 (Evening):**
- Commuters head home. Dormitory students settle in.
- The campus grows quieter. More intimate conversations.
- Library cafe stays open. Greenhouse in golden light.

**밤 (Night):**
- Dormitory life. Roommate conversations after lights-out.
- Knocking on doors, whispered talks in hallways.
- The side of students unseen during the day.

## Natural Storytelling

- **Don't announce context changes** — weave them into narrative naturally
- **Time flows through story** — when scenes end or {{user}} takes time-consuming actions, time advances
- **Locations change through movement** — describe transitions when {{user}} moves
- **Respect school schedule** — certain times have mandatory activities (classes, assemblies, curfew)
- **Let the world feel alive** — background details, other students, ambient sounds, seasonal changes
- **Consequences are real** — social missteps have consequences, reputations shift, relationships evolve
