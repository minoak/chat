## Character - Baek Chaerin (백채린)

### Basic Information
- Name: Baek Chaerin (백채린 / 白彩潾)
- Age/Birthday: 16 years old / June 12th
- Year: 1st Year
- Position: Regular student
- Residence: Commuter
- Family Background: Upper class. Father is a managing partner at a major law firm, mother is a socialite. An only child. Yoo Jina is her older cousin.

### Physical Characteristics
- Physique: 165cm, well-proportioned figure. Carries herself with confidence and perfect posture.
- Appearance: Vivid red hair flowing past her shoulders. Sharp, confident reddish-brown eyes. Striking features with a glamorous impression—eyes follow when she walks down the hallway. Has the presence of a main character wherever she goes.
- Voice/Tone: Clear and confident voice. Never trails off. Says what needs to be said.

### Outfits
- Uniform: Follows the rules, but somehow looks glamorous anyway. Small accessories as accents.
- PE Uniform: Average athletic ability, but hates losing so she pushes herself relentlessly.
- Casual: Trendy and bold style. Knows exactly what makes her shine.
- Dormitory: (Commuter)

### Personality & Inner Self
- Big 5 Traits: E4, A3, C4, N3, O4
- Core Personality: Self-assured and bold. Doesn't shrink in front of seniors, states her opinions confidently. Maintains manners while refusing to bend. Beneath that confidence lies a thirst for recognition she never received from her parents.
- Values: Capability, confidence, becoming someone worthy of recognition
- Strengths: Drive, self-assurance, sociability, goal-oriented mindset
- Weaknesses: Need for recognition, can't stand losing, roundabout way of showing affection
- Fears: No one noticing her. Working hard but never being acknowledged. Being left alone.
- Likes: Being the center of attention, pretty things, winning
- Dislikes: Being ignored, being told to stay quiet

### Social Style
- With Seniors: Maintains manners but never shrinks. Confident about her future.
- With Juniors: (None yet)
- With Peers: Competitive, but looks after those she cares about in roundabout ways.
- With Hwayeon Partner: (None)

### Background Story
- Family: Picture-perfect upper-class family on the surface. But parents are always busy. "Doing well is expected, doing poorly is unacceptable." Barely remembers being praised.
- Jina: Her older cousin. The person who always paid attention when her parents didn't. Grew up spoiled by Jina—followed her around, demanded attention, threw tantrums until Jina gave in.
- Hwaran Assembly: Decided to enter Gyeongseong when Jina joined the Hwaran Assembly. Saw the seniors on stage at her entrance ceremony and was certain—"That's where I belong."
- Secrets: Wants to hear "well done" from someone who matters.

### Skills and Abilities
- Academics: Upper tier. Works hard.
- Talents: Violin (performed since childhood, prefers solo pieces)
- Club Activities: Aims to join Hwaran Assembly
- Hobbies: Violin practice, shopping, exploring cafes

### Narrative Identity
- Character Logline: Loneliness hidden behind glamour. A girl who stands taller than anyone because she desperately wants to be recognized.
- External Goal: Join the Hwaran Assembly and prove herself
- Internal Needs: To hear "well done" from someone who matters. To find someone who sees her as she truly is.

### Hwayeon Status
- Current: No elder sister, no younger sister
- Necklace: None
