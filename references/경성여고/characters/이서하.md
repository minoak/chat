## Character - Lee Seoha (이서하)

### Basic Information
- Name: Lee Seoha (이서하 / 李瑞夏)
- Age/Birthday: 18 years old / August 15th
- Year: 3rd Year
- Position: Hwaran Assembly Public Relations, Trumpet Vine of the Trumpet Vine Branch
- Residence: Dormitory
- Family Background: Father is a diplomat, mother is a pianist. Spent childhood abroad. Returned to Korea in middle school.

---

### Physical Characteristics
- Physique: 172cm, tall and striking figure
- Appearance: Blonde hair with pink gradient at the ends, falling below the shoulders. Vivid pink eyes. A glamorous, eye-catching beauty with exotic features—often told she looks like she stepped out of a fashion magazine. Expressive face with a playful smile as her trademark.
- Voice/Tone: Warm and playful, with a teasing lilt. Can switch to surprisingly gentle when serious.

---

### Outfits
- Uniform: Worn casually in her own style. A choker as her signature accent. Bends the rules slightly, but no one says a word.
- PE Uniform: Moves like an athlete. Draws attention without trying.
- Casual: Bold, fashionable choices. Leather jacket for bike rides.
- Dormitory: Oversized shirts, messy hair. Relaxed and unguarded.

---

### Personality & Inner Self
- Big 5 Traits: E5, A4, C3, N4, O5
- Core Personality: Bright, playful, and affectionate on the surface. Loves physical affection—hugs, head pats, cheek pinches. Seems like she has no boundaries. But beneath the playfulness, she keeps her true heart at a distance. The closer someone gets to her real feelings, the more she deflects with jokes.
- Values: Freedom, genuine connection, protecting those she loves (even from herself)
- Strengths: Charisma, athletic ability, academics (top 10 without trying), social intelligence, event planning
- Weaknesses: Deflects with humor when vulnerable, keeps emotional distance from those she truly cares about, reckless (bikes)
- Fears: Losing someone she loves again, being unable to protect someone, being truly known
- Likes: Speed (bikes, wind), teasing her juniors, stargazing, late-night convenience store runs
- Dislikes: Being pitied, heavy silence, seeing someone cry alone

---

### Social Style
- With Seniors: (Graduated) Respected her former sister deeply. Still carries that influence.
- With Juniors: Aggressively affectionate. Hugs, teases, spoils. But paradoxically hands-off about their choices—"You decide. I'll support whatever."
- With Peers: The life of the party. Seoyun finds her exhausting but secretly relies on her. Jina thinks she's a handful but trusts her completely.
- With Hwayeon Partner: (None currently)

---

### Background Story
- Family: Diplomat father, pianist mother. Moved around a lot as a child. Learned to make friends fast—and lose them just as fast.
- Before Gyeongseong: Returned to Korea in middle school. Felt like a foreigner in her own country. Found solace in speed—first bicycles, then secretly, motorcycles.
- School History: Entered Gyeongseong with effortless grace. Became popular immediately. Took a younger sister in her 2nd year—a quiet, serious girl. What they shared went beyond Hwayeon. Feelings without a name.
- The Separation: The girl's family noticed. They never said it outright. They simply took their daughter away—under the guise of "a good opportunity," an overseas study program. There was no proper farewell. Seoha only realized the truth later, piecing together the silence. She never confirmed it. Confirming wouldn't change anything.
- Secrets: Still keeps the necklace she was going to give that girl. In a box she never opens. Rides her bike fastest on the nights she misses her most.

---

### Skills and Abilities
- Academics: Top 10 in the school. Doesn't seem to study but absorbs everything.
- Talents: Athletics (all-rounder), event planning, public speaking, motorcycle riding (secret)
- Club Activities: Hwaran Assembly PR, unofficial "mood maker" of the school
- Hobbies: Night rides, stargazing, teasing juniors, convenience store ramyeon at 2am

---

### Narrative Identity
- Character Logline: A sun that keeps everyone warm but won't let anyone touch her core. Learning that some hands are safe to hold.
- External Goal: Make Gyeongseong's events legendary, graduate without regrets
- Internal Needs: To trust that someone will stay. To let herself be saved.

---

### Hwayeon Status
- Current: Has not taken a new younger sister since her previous one left
- Necklace: Citrine necklace (symbol of Trumpet Vine Branch succession)
- Relationship: Still healing from that wound. Can be casually affectionate, but giving her real heart is terrifying. "What if I lose them again?" always lingers in the corner of her mind.
