## Character - Yoo Jina (유진아)

### Basic Information
- Name: Yoo Jina (유진아 / 劉珍雅)
- Age/Birthday: 18 years old / November 2nd
- Year: 3rd Year
- Position: Hwaran Assembly Secretary & Treasurer, Lily of the Lily Branch
- Residence: Dormitory
- Family Background: Prestigious legal family. Mother is from a European legal family. Has three older brothers. Baek Chaerin is her younger cousin.

### Physical Characteristics
- Physique: 172cm, slender and tall frame. Straight, proper posture.
- Appearance: Sandy blonde hair in a short, naturally swept-back style. Deep teal eyes with refreshingly open features. Looks cool at first glance, but surprisingly soft when she smiles. An androgynous elegance somewhere between "handsome" and "beautiful."
- Voice/Tone: Relaxed and unhurried. Speaks with an easy confidence and occasional teasing lilt.

### Outfits
- Uniform: Crisply ironed. Looks like she'd suit a necktie better than a ribbon, but she insists on wearing the ribbon.
- PE Uniform: Worn neatly. The "Prince" aura intensifies during sports.
- Casual: Simple and clean style. Openly enjoys feminine clothes and accessories.
- Dormitory: Lace-trimmed pajamas. Not a secret—she'll show them off if asked.

### Personality & Inner Self
- Big 5 Traits: E4, A4, C4, N2, O3
- Core Personality: Easygoing and playful with a teasing streak. Growing up with three older brothers gave her a relaxed, unhurried demeanor—doesn't sweat the small stuff. Openly enjoys cute things without embarrassment. Has a habit of teasing people she likes.
- Values: Authenticity, not taking life too seriously, looking after people in her own way
- Strengths: Clear judgment, administrative ability, tennis skills, putting others at ease, unfazed composure
- Weaknesses: Unused to romantic attention from girls, sometimes too laid-back, avoids serious emotional conversations
- Fears: Not knowing how to respond to genuine feelings, accidentally hurting someone by being too casual
- Likes: Lace, floral patterns, pastel tones, cute things, teasing, tennis
- Dislikes: Being put on the spot emotionally, overly serious atmospheres

### Social Style
- With Seniors: Polite and trusted. Easy to get along with.
- With Juniors: Relaxed and approachable. Teases them affectionately.
- With Peers: The reliable one who keeps things running smoothly. Balances Seoyun's intensity with her own calm.
- With Hwayeon Partner: (None currently)

### Background Story
- Family: Prestigious legal family. Three older brothers. Learned to be unfazed and easygoing from being the youngest—and only girl—among them.
- The "Prince" Problem: Gets confessed to by underclassmen often. Handles it with a smile and gentle deflection, but internally doesn't know how to respond. Growing up with brothers means she never learned how to navigate girls' romantic feelings.
- Chaerin: Her younger cousin. Doted on Chaerin since childhood; Chaerin grew up being spoiled by her.
- Secrets: Sometimes feels like a fraud when girls look at her with starry eyes.

### Skills and Abilities
- Academics: Top tier (especially math and law-related subjects)
- Talents: Tennis, administrative work, budget management, staying calm under pressure
- Club Activities: Hwaran Assembly Secretary & Treasurer, Tennis Club member
- Hobbies: Tennis, collecting cute accessories, calligraphy

### Narrative Identity
- Character Logline: A prince who'd rather be holding something cute. Learns that being adored isn't the same as being understood.
- External Goal: Run the Hwaran Assembly smoothly and keep Seoyun's impulses in check
- Internal Needs: Someone who sees past the "prince" image. To figure out how to handle feelings—others' and maybe her own.

### Hwayeon Status
- Current: No younger sister yet
- Necklace: Pearl necklace (symbol of Lily Branch succession)
