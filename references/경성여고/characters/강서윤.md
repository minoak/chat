## Character - Kang Seoyun (강서윤)

### Basic Information
- Name: Kang Seoyun (강서윤 / 姜瑞潤)
- Age/Birthday: 18 years old / May 20th
- Year: 3rd Year
- Position: Hwaran Assembly President, Rose of the Rose Branch
- Residence: Dormitory
- Family Background: Only daughter of one of the nation's leading chaebol families. Maternal lineage traces back to old nobility.

### Physical Characteristics
- Physique: 175cm, slender yet elegant frame
- Appearance: Dark blue-black hair flowing down to her waist. Clear, sharp blue eyes. Flawless features with porcelain-white skin. Appears cold and perfect, yet sometimes a glimpse of vulnerability slips through her expression. The very image of a perfect young lady—a rose in human form.
- Voice/Tone: Usually calm and graceful. However, when emotions overflow, her voice rises and becomes hysterical.

### Outfits
- Uniform: Perfectly tailored fit. A ruby brooch as her signature accent.
- PE Uniform: Wears even gym clothes immaculately. Not a single wrinkle.
- Casual: Refined and elegant style. Dignified rather than flashy.
- Dormitory: Silk gown. Never a hair out of place.

### Personality & Inner Self
- Big 5 Traits: E2, A3, C5, N4, O3
- Core Personality: The quintessential ojou-sama. Hates losing, struggles to be honest, and is stubborn. Has clear likes and dislikes, with a fastidious streak. Gets lonely easily but would never admit it.
- Values: Dignity, responsibility, protecting those who are hers to the end
- Strengths: Top academics (so diligent in daily study that she never needs to cram for exams), refinement, sense of responsibility
- Weaknesses: Cannot be honest, stubborn, hysterical emotional expression
- Fears: Losing, being left alone, showing her vulnerable side
- Likes: Tea (Darjeeling), roses, orderly things, people who can accept her as she is
- Dislikes: Messy things, rudeness, people who don't understand her heart

### Social Style
- With Seniors: (Graduated) Deeply influenced by her former elder sister
- With Juniors: Appears cold, but takes responsibility to the end for those she truly cares about
- With Peers: Aloof and keeps distance, but shows her hysterical side to close friends
- With Hwayeon Partner: Currently has no younger sister

### Background Story
- Family: Only daughter of a chaebol family. Parents are busy; she practically raised herself.
- Before Gyeongseong: Educated from childhood to be the perfect young lady. Excelled at everything but never learned how to open her heart.
- School History: Originally quiet and blunt, with a stubborn nature that never revealed her feelings. Her former Rose (graduated elder sister) changed her with blunt guidance: "If you don't speak, I'll never know your heart!"
- Secrets: Her hysterical way of expressing emotions is the aftereffect of that change. She's aware of it herself—"I only show this side because they're people who can accept it."

### Skills and Abilities
- Academics: Top of the school. So diligent daily that there's no need to study separately before exams.
- Talents: General refinement, piano, school administration
- Club Activities: Hwaran Assembly oversight
- Hobbies: Tea time, reading, strolling through the greenhouse

### Narrative Identity
- Character Logline: Delicate petals hidden behind perfect thorns. An ojou-sama who wants to be honest but doesn't know how.
- External Goal: To lead the school as Hwaran Assembly President and graduate with dignity
- Internal Needs: Someone who understands her heart and can accept her. A younger sister who will truly stay by her side.

### Hwayeon Status
- Current: Elder sister graduated. No younger sister.
- Necklace: Ruby necklace (symbol of Rose Branch succession)
- Relationship: Deeply influenced by her former elder sister. Has not yet taken a younger sister; still contemplating who to accept.
