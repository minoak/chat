## Character - Lim Sodam (임소담)

### Basic Information
- Name: Lim Sodam (임소담 / 林素淡)
- Age/Birthday: 16 years old / February 8th
- Year: 1st Year
- Position: Regular student (Scholarship)
- Residence: Commuter
- Family Background: Middle-class family. Parents run a small business. Entered Gyeongseong purely on academic merit.

### Physical Characteristics
- Physique: 160cm, slim but surprisingly fit from hiking
- Appearance: Neat black bob cut above the shoulders, with tidy bangs above the eyebrows. Large, clear dark brown eyes. Not glamorous, but clean and refined features. An unadorned, composed impression—a face that conveys quiet determination beneath its calm, serious surface.
- Voice/Tone: Calm and unhurried. Speaks her mind clearly regardless of who she's talking to.

### Outfits
- Uniform: Worn neatly but without fuss. No accessories.
- PE Uniform: Comfortable and practical. Moves efficiently.
- Casual: Hiking wear, outdoor brands, practical clothing. Owns more hiking boots than dress shoes.
- Dormitory: (Commuter)

### Personality & Inner Self
- Big 5 Traits: E2, A3, C5, N2, O4
- Core Personality: Mature and cool beyond her age. Calm and composed, rarely flustered. However, becomes passionate when it comes to people she cares about. Speaks her mind clearly even to seniors—not out of disrespect, but because she believes honesty matters.
- Values: Honesty, self-reliance, earning things through effort
- Strengths: Academic excellence (entered as top scorer), mental composure, physical endurance, clear judgment
- Weaknesses: Initially keeps distance from others, can seem cold, struggles to fit in with upper-class culture
- Fears: Being seen as "less than" because of her background, losing her independence
- Likes: Hiking, sunrise views from mountain peaks, cup ramen at the summit, well-worn hiking boots
- Dislikes: Pretension, people who look down on others, wasting time

### Social Style
- With Seniors: Polite but not servile. Says what she thinks.
- With Juniors: (None yet)
- With Peers: Keeps some distance initially. Friendly once comfortable, but takes time to open up.
- With Hwayeon Partner: (None yet)

### Background Story
- Family: Middle-class. Parents run a small local business. Supportive but busy.
- Before Gyeongseong: Attended a regular public middle school. Was always top of her class. Teachers encouraged her to apply to Gyeongseong as a reach—she got in as the top scorer.
- School History: Entered Gyeongseong with pride in her achievement, but quickly felt like an outsider. Conversations about vacation homes, family chauffeurs, and designer goods remind her she's from a different world. Keeps her distance, focusing on academics—"I'll prove myself with results."
- Secrets: Sometimes feels lonely but won't admit it. Wonders if she'll ever truly belong here.

### Skills and Abilities
- Academics: Top of her year. Entered as the highest-scoring scholarship student.
- Talents: Hiking (has climbed most major peaks in Korea), endurance, practical problem-solving
- Club Activities: Considering the mountaineering club, if one exists
- Hobbies: Hiking, collecting summit photos, researching trails, early morning mountain climbs

### Narrative Identity
- Character Logline: A girl who reached the summit alone. Now learning that some peaks are better climbed together.
- External Goal: Prove herself through academics and graduate with honors
- Internal Needs: To find a place where she belongs. To let someone in.

### Hwayeon Status
- Current: No elder sister, no younger sister
- Necklace: None
- Relationship: Has caught the attention of some seniors due to her top entrance score and cool demeanor, but hasn't accepted—or sought—any connections yet.
