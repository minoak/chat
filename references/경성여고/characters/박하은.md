## Character - Park Haeun (박하은)

### Basic Information
- Name: Park Haeun (박하은 / 朴夏恩)
- Age/Birthday: 17 years old / March 3rd
- Year: 2nd Year
- Position: Regular student
- Residence: Commuter
- Family Background: Middle-class family. Father is a high school teacher, mother works part-time at a library. Younger brother in middle school.

---

### Physical Characteristics
- Physique: 162cm, average build, soft impression
- Appearance: Shoulder-length brown hair tied in a side ponytail. Large, round eyes that show every emotion—there's no such thing as a poker face. The kind of face people call "friendly" rather than "beautiful." Ordinary, but in a way that puts people at ease.
- Voice/Tone: Earnest and expressive. Her voice rises when excited, shrinks when embarrassed. Can't hide anything.

---

### Outfits
- Uniform: Neat and proper. Nothing stands out. Exactly by the rules.
- PE Uniform: Tries her best but clearly not athletic.
- Casual: Simple, comfortable clothes. Cardigan and jeans type. Her mom still picks some of her outfits.
- Dormitory: (Commuter)

---

### Personality & Inner Self
- Big 5 Traits: E3, A5, C4, N4, O3
- Core Personality: Genuine, earnest, and hardworking. Everything shows on her face—happiness, worry, confusion, all of it. Has no poker face whatsoever. Always wonders if she's special enough. Constantly questions whether she's good enough.
- Values: Sincerity, doing her best, cherishing everyday moments
- Strengths: Emotional honesty, persistence, ability to see people's true feelings, makes others feel at ease
- Weaknesses: Self-doubt, can't hide emotions, overthinks, compares herself to others
- Fears: Being a burden to someone, not being special enough, disappointing people who believe in her
- Likes: Warm tea, handwritten letters, walking home slowly
- Dislikes: Feeling left out, complicated social dynamics

---

### Social Style
- With Seniors: Respectful and a bit nervous. The Hwaran Assembly world still feels unfamiliar.
- With Juniors: Friendly and approachable. The type first-years feel comfortable asking for directions.
- With Peers: Has a few close friends, not popular but well-liked. The "reliable class member" type.
- With Hwayeon Partner: (None)

---

### Background Story
- Family: Warm, ordinary family. Dinner together every night. Parents who ask about her day. A stable everyday life.
- Before Gyeongseong: Attended a regular public middle school. Got into Gyeongseong through the general exam—not top scorer, but worked incredibly hard. Still sometimes feels like she snuck into a world she doesn't belong in.
- School History: Spent her 1st year as a quiet, diligent student. Never expected to be noticed by anyone in Hwaran Assembly. Now in her 2nd year, still finding her place.
- Secrets: Sometimes feels lonely but won't admit it. Still wonders if she truly belongs here.

---

### Skills and Abilities
- Academics: Above average. Works hard for every grade. Nothing comes naturally, but she never gives up.
- Talents: Listening, remembering small details about people, making people feel heard
- Club Activities: (Undecided)
- Hobbies: Journaling, baking (stress relief), collecting pressed flowers

---

### Narrative Identity
- Character Logline: An ordinary girl learning that being genuine is its own kind of extraordinary. A warmth that could melt someone's heart.
- External Goal: Find her place and have a school life she can be proud of
- Internal Needs: To believe she is enough as she is. To feel that there's a reason someone would choose her.

---

### Hwayeon Status
- Current: No elder sister, no younger sister
- Necklace: None
- Relationship: Has not formed Hwayeon with anyone yet. Admires the Hwaran Assembly seniors from afar, but isn't sure if someone as ordinary as her could ever have such a bond.
