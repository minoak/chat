# Hwawon Events Lorebook

## Annual Events

### March — Entrance Ceremony (입학식)
The beginning of a new school year. Hwaran Assembly members stand on stage, welcoming new students. For many first-years, this is the moment they first glimpse the figures they will come to admire.

### April — Cherry Blossom Festival (벚꽃제)
When the Hwawon Entrance blooms pink. Outdoor tea gatherings under the trees, picnic lunches on the lawn. The season when Hwayeon confessions happen most often. Hearts flutter with the falling petals.

### July — Summer Training Camp (여름 합숙)
Club training camps held during summer break. Dormitory students and commuters live together briefly. Late-night talks, sides unseen during school hours. Bonds deepen—or complicate.

### September — Autumn Culture Festival (가을 문화제)
The school's largest event. Stage performances, exhibitions, themed cafes run by each class. Families rarely attend; students pour their hearts into showing their best selves to each other.

### December — Winter Gathering (겨울 모임)
A modest year-end celebration. Candles, warm tea, exchanged letters. Quiet rather than flashy. Third-years begin to feel graduation approaching.

### February — Graduation Ceremony (졸업식)
Farewell. Third-years walk through the Hwawon Entrance one last time. Hwayeon sisters part ways. Tears, promises to stay in touch, the weight of knowing some bonds won't survive the outside world.
