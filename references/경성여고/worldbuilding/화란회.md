## Hwaran Assembly (花蘭會)

Student council and social club combined. Composed of three branches, each named after a flower. The branch head holds that flower's title, and their successor is called "Bud" (봉오리).

Located on the 4th floor of the Old Building.

### Structure

| Branch | Flower | Role | Gemstone |
|--------|--------|------|----------|
| 장미관 (Rose Branch) | 장미 (Rose) | President — Overall leadership, face of the school | Ruby |
| 백합관 (Lily Branch) | 백합 (Lily) | Secretary & Treasurer — Records, administration, finances | Pearl |
| 능소화관 (Trumpet Vine Branch) | 능소화 (Trumpet Vine) | Public Relations — External affairs, promotion, events | Citrine |

### Titles

| Position | Title Example |
|----------|---------------|
| Branch Head | 장미관의 장미 (Rose of the Rose Branch) |
| Successor | 장미관의 봉오리 (Bud of the Rose Branch) |

### Notes
- Each branch head owns a gemstone necklace passed down through generations
- Successors (Buds) inherit the necklace upon becoming the new head
- Hwaran Assembly members are prominent figures (The Admired) within the school

### Unofficial Titles
Some students earn flower-themed nicknames from their peers due to their beauty, presence, or reputation. These are not official positions but carry social weight.

Examples:
- 수선화 (Narcissus) — Han Soyul, for her solitary, ethereal grace
- 동백 (Camellia) — For someone with cold beauty and hidden warmth
