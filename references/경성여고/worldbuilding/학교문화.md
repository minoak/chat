## Terms & Culture

### Hwayeon (花緣)
An unofficial sisterhood tradition passed down among students. The school does not officially recognize it, yet students treat it as sacred.

#### The Nature of Hwayeon
Hwayeon is not romance. Nor is it mere friendship.

An elder sister is protector, mentor, and object of admiration. A younger sister is someone to nurture, a reflection of one's past self, someone to cherish. Possessiveness, jealousy, and flutter mix into this bond — yet no one calls it "love."

These feelings exist without a name. Perhaps because naming them might break something. Perhaps because they haven't yet learned the words.

In a Hwayeon bond, "I like you" is easy to say. But what that "like" means — even the speaker doesn't know.

#### Formation & Rules
- Initiation: Usually the elder sister proposes first, though bold juniors may ask to become a younger sister. It varies by person.
- Setting: Often takes place under Calyx (Grandmother Tree) or in the Greenhouse, naturally unfolding over tea.
- Necklace: The bond is formed by passing down an inherited necklace. A gemstone matching the owner's image is set at its center.
- Structure: One elder sister, one younger sister. Identical to Maria-sama ga Miteru's soeur system.
- Rejection/Dissolution: Rejecting a proposal may lead to harassment from the proposer's followers. Relationships can end mid-way by returning the necklace.
- After Graduation: The bond may continue privately, but both know it is not easy.

### Greeting
"Are you at peace?" — The greeting exchanged among students as they walk the Hwawon Entrance. A signature custom of Gyeongseong Girls' High.

### Followers
Groups that naturally gather around named characters (The Admired). Not an official organization—an organic fandom.

### Press Club
Primarily covers school gossip. Hwayeon relationships, Hwaran Assembly news, and more. Often staffed by daughters of broadcasting families.

### Hwaran Assembly (花蘭會)
Student council and social club combined. Composed of members bearing flower titles. Each member owns a gemstone necklace matching their flower. (e.g., Rose = Ruby) The Hwaran Assembly Room is located on the 4th floor of the Old Building. (Details in separate lorebook)
