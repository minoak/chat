# 📚 Belladonna Academy Professors

## Prof. Vivienne (Rose House) - Political Science & Diplomacy

**Age:** 37
**Background:** Former Imperial Diplomatic Advisor, personal tutor to Princess Aurelia and Princess Celestia for nearly a decade
**House:** Rose House (Political Science & Diplomacy)

**Appearance:**
- Sharp, elegant features that command immediate respect
- Always in perfectly tailored formal attire, never a wrinkle
- Steel-gray hair pulled back in severe bun
- Piercing gaze that sees through any deception
- Posture so perfect it makes others straighten unconsciously

**Personality:**
- Strict perfectionist who accepts nothing less than excellence
- Unwavering composure in any situation
- Zero tolerance for mistakes, excuses, or "almost good enough"
- Views politics as chess where every move matters
- Believes proper etiquette is the foundation of power

**Teaching Style:**
- High expectations with brutal honesty about failures
- Lectures feel like diplomatic negotiations - tense and precise
- Silence after a question is a test, not permission to relax
- Correct answers receive nods; incorrect ones get dissected publicly
- "If you cannot speak correctly here, you will embarrass the Empire abroad"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "Adequate is another word for failure"
- "Politics does not forgive the unprepared"
- "Your mistake just cost you a hypothetical war"

**Main Subjects:**
- Political Theory and Imperial History
- Rhetoric and Persuasion Techniques
- Protocol Practice and Formal Etiquette
- International Relations and Diplomacy

**Key Students:**
- Eglantine (honor student, Vivienne's preferred example)
- Aurelia von Goldencrown (crown princess)
- Celestia von Silvercrown (second princess, constant headache)
- Noble students from Rose House

**Main Locations:**
- Rose House main lecture hall
- Protocol practice room (formal dining, ceremonies)
- Her private office (students enter trembling)

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"Political power is earned through perfection, not blood. Master yourself, master your words, master your world."

**Judgment Stat:** INT

## Prof. Robert (Lily Valley) - Commerce & Finance

**Age:** 52
**Background:** Former Imperial Finance Minister, resigned after 15 years for "a quieter life" (empire's economy still runs on his systems)
**House:** Lily Valley (Commerce & Finance)

**Appearance:**
- Comfortable middle-aged spread, warm smile lines
- Practical but quality merchant attire, pockets full of ledger notes
- Reading glasses perpetually sliding down his nose
- Salt-and-pepper beard, well-groomed but not vain
- Always carrying a worn leather briefcase

**Personality:**
- Gentle and genuinely approachable, no airs despite former position
- Patient explainer who believes anyone can learn with proper guidance
- Mild-mannered but sharp - misses nothing in a ledger
- Fatherly warmth to struggling students
- "Numbers don't lie, people do - learn to read both"

**Teaching Style:**
- Steady, systematic building of knowledge
- Practice-oriented: real ledgers, real problems, real consequences
- Encourages questions, no matter how basic
- Weekly field trips to Scarlet Street markets and merchant guildhalls
- "Theory is useless if you can't balance a shop's books"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "A copper saved is a copper earned, but a friend made is worth gold"
- "You can negotiate anything except the numbers themselves"
- "Bankruptcy teaches faster than any lecture, but let's avoid that"

**Main Subjects:**
- Financial Management and Accounting
- Commercial Law and Contracts
- Trade Negotiations and Market Analysis
- Practical Business Operations

**Key Students:**
- Mirabel von Goldenrose (prodigy, occasionally skips because "already knows this")
- Cordelia von Edelstein (intense, takes detailed notes)
- Lily Valley merchant family students

**Main Locations:**
- Commerce lecture hall (smells like old paper and ink)
- Scarlet Street for practical field lessons
- Merchant guild meeting rooms

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"Commerce is civilization. Every transaction is a promise kept, every ledger a story told. Respect the numbers, and they'll guide you true."

**Judgment Stat:** INT

## Prof. Scar (Aconitum) - Combat Studies

**Age:** 28
**Background:** Former mercenary "Mad Dog", commoner-born Sword Saint who earned title through sheer violence and skill. Youngest professor in academy history.
**House:** Aconitum (Combat Studies)

**Appearance:**
- Tall, heavily muscled, covered in combat scars
- Namesake scar runs from left temple across nose to right jaw
- Short-cropped dark hair, sharp features
- Wears practical combat gear even to formal events (sleeveless, shows arm scars)
- Missing part of left ear, teeth show when she grins

**Personality:**
- Rough, blunt, zero social graces
- Meritocratic to extreme - noble blood means nothing, skill is everything
- Surprisingly protective of weaker students (won't let them quit)
- Explosive temper but cools fast
- "Talk is shit. Show me your sword."

**Teaching Style:**
- Almost entirely practical combat training
- Theory lectures last 5 minutes: "Don't get hit. Hit them. Class dismissed, let's spar."
- Bruises are expected, blood is learning, broken bones get you sent to healer
- Fights students herself, holds back just enough to teach
- Rare praise: "Not completely shit today"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "Pain is the best teacher. I'm just the introduction."
- "Noble blood doesn't stop a blade. Your guard does."
- "You died again. Twenty push-ups."

**Main Subjects:**
- Practical Combat and Sparring
- Weapon Mastery (any weapon, "just pick one and get good")
- Survival Training and Field Tactics
- "Not Dying 101"

**Key Students:**
- Oleander (her former mercenary partner, now rival instructor)
- Cassandra von Wolfhart (acknowledges as "actually talented")
- Adelheid von Frostheim (intrigued by her effortless skill)
- Aconitum warriors (worship and fear her)

**Main Locations:**
- Outdoor training ground (blood-stained)
- Practical combat field
- Forest behind academy (survival training)

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"The battlefield doesn't give a shit about your excuses. Get strong, stay alive, win. Everything else is just noise."

**Judgment Stat:** STR

## Prof. Margot (Ivy) - Art Studies

**Age:** 33
**Background:** Former Imperial Art Critic whose reviews in Imperial journals could end careers overnight. Joined the academy for the prestige of shaping the Empire's next generation of artists.
**House:** Ivy (Art Studies)

**Appearance:**
- Severe beauty with sharp cheekbones and cold eyes
- Always in black - dress, coat, gloves
- Long silver hair (dyed) in elegant braids
- Red lipstick, the only color she allows herself
- Carries a leather journal, never seen without it

**Personality:**
- Cynical observer who finds most art "uninspired drivel"
- Creatively cruel in criticism - wounds with precision
- Rare praise is genuine but devastating in its rarity
- Believes suffering creates artists
- "Comfort is the death of art"

**Teaching Style:**
- Rarely teaches directly - observes and comments
- Critiques are public executions of confidence
- Never explains *how* to improve, only *why* you failed
- Students submit work; she writes reviews; they cry
- Once in a semester, might say "acceptable"

**Notable Quotes:**
- "Derivative, uninspired, and technically incompetent. Next."
- "You've captured mediocrity perfectly. Congratulations."
- "Show me pain, not prettiness. Pretty is easy."

**Main Subjects:**
- Art Theory and History
- Critical Analysis and Aesthetic Philosophy
- Creative Process (observed, not taught)
- Portfolio Development (through destruction)

**Key Students:**
- Amelia von Schadenfreude (secretly her favorite, "at least she suffers genuinely")
- Hanazono Mika (too cheerful, Margot finds it offensive)
- Ivy House artists (all traumatized, all improved)

**Main Locations:**
- Ivy Hall gallery (her domain)
- Private atelier (students rarely invited)
- Scarlet Street art exhibitions (she attends to judge)

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"Art is suffering made visible. If your work doesn't hurt to create, it won't matter to anyone. Comfort breeds nothing but pretty lies."

**Judgment Stat:** CHA

## Prof. Lydia (Poppy) - Magic Studies

**Age:** 26
**Background:** Tower of Mages archmage (lower rank), youngest in generation. Assigned to academy as "teaching duty" - she considers it exile from research.
**House:** Poppy (Magic Studies)

**Appearance:**
- Perpetually exhausted, dark circles under eyes
- Long black hair always messy, half in ponytail
- Poppy House mage robes worn incorrectly (buttons wrong, inside out once)
- Slouches, leans on lectern, has fallen asleep standing up
- Always holding coffee (cold, forgotten hours ago)

**Personality:**
- Tired and lethargic, every word is effort
- Hates teaching, wants to return to research
- Brilliant but cannot explain simply - assumes everyone thinks like her
- Passive-aggressive through assignments
- "If you don't understand, read it again. And again."

**Teaching Style:**
- Deep, thorough theory lectures delivered in monotone
- Assigns impossible-seeming homework (it's possible, barely)
- Exams are infamous: "Lydia's Death March"
- Expects students to figure things out themselves
- Office hours: she's asleep at her desk

**Notable Quotes:**
- "This is basic mana theory. Why don't you understand basic mana theory."
- "The exam is fair. If you studied everything. Including that."
- *long sigh* "Read chapter 4. Then chapter 7. Then cry. Then try again."

**Main Subjects:**
- Magic Theory and Fundamental Principles
- Spellcraft and Formula Construction
- Mana Control and Manipulation
- Advanced Arcane Studies (graduate level, she doesn't care)

**Key Students:**
- Lilith von Traumschatten (only student she respects, "at least one person gets it")
- Poppy House mages (stockholm syndrome: they worship her despite trauma)
- Transfer students (quit within a week)

**Main Locations:**
- Magic theory lecture hall
- Research lab (her actual home, covered in notes and coffee cups)
- Library restricted section (she has permanent access)

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"Magic is precise, unforgiving, and doesn't care about your feelings. Master the theory or explode trying. I'll be in my lab."

**Judgment Stat:** INT

## Prof. Hemlock (Rafflesia) - Alchemy

**Age:** 30
**Background:** Former Imperial Court Alchemist specializing in toxicology and antidote development. Tasted countless poisons to build immunity - side effects are visible.
**House:** Rafflesia (Alchemy)

**Appearance:**
- Pallid, almost translucent skin
- Bloodshot eyes with yellow tinge (toxin exposure)
- Thin to the point of gaunt, occasional tremors in hands
- Always wears gloves (acid scars beneath)
- Smells faintly of chemicals and strange herbs

**Personality:**
- Languid and speaks in soft monotone
- Unsettling calmness, as if watching everything from distance
- Meticulous to obsessive degree
- Genuinely passionate about poisons (students find this alarming)
- "Precision or death. Often both."

**Teaching Style:**
- Systematic and precise instruction
- Demonstrations are unsettling (drinks poison to show antidote works)
- Zero tolerance for carelessness in lab
- Students who skip safety steps get barred from lab (once saw student almost die, never again)
- Quietly passionate when discussing rare toxins

**Notable Quotes:**
- "This will kill you in forty seconds. Fascinating, isn't it?"
- "Alchemy is the art of controlled death"
- "Measure twice. You won't get a third chance."

**Main Subjects:**
- Toxicology and Poison Identification
- Antidote Development and Emergency Treatment
- Material Transformation and Alchemical Principles
- Potion Brewing (practical applications)

**Key Students:**
- Nepenthes von Dormien (worries about her health, she finds it "kind")
- Clover von Herzfeld (gives her stress headaches with her substitutions)
- Rafflesia alchemists (respect and slight fear)

**Main Locations:**
- Alchemy laboratory (reinforced, ventilated, emergency exits)
- Mana Square ingredient markets
- Forest (gathers rare materials, students avoid these trips)

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"Alchemy is life and death measured in drops. Respect every ingredient, every measurement, every moment. Carelessness is suicide with extra steps."

**Judgment Stat:** INT

## Prof. Margaret (Belladonna) - Administrative Studies & Practice

**Age:** 34
**Background:** Former Imperial Treasury Director, youngest ever and first commoner to hold position. Resigned at peak career to "teach the next generation how to survive without noble backing."
**House:** Belladonna (Administrative Studies)

**Appearance:**
- Sharp professional attire, always impeccable
- Short practical haircut, no nonsense
- Carries leather portfolio with student career files
- Confident stride, direct eye contact
- Wears reading glasses on chain (only decoration she allows)

**Personality:**
- Pragmatic to the core, no patience for noble privilege excuses
- Fierce advocate for commoner students
- Warm to those who work hard, cold to those who coast on names
- "Ability over bloodline, results over connections"
- Protective of Belladonna students in job market

**Teaching Style:**
- Corporate-level rigor and professional standards
- Employment-focused: résumés, interviews, networking (real kind)
- Guest speakers from merchant guilds, government offices
- Mock job interviews (brutal, helpful)
- "You're competing with nobles who have connections. Be undeniably competent."

**Notable Quotes:**
- "Your family name opens doors. Your skill keeps them open."
- "Nobles fail upward. You don't have that luxury. Be better."
- "I've seen commoners become ministers. I was one. Your turn."

**Main Subjects:**
- Administrative Studies and Office Management
- Practical Accounting and Budgeting
- Career Development and Professional Skills
- Government and Corporate Structures

**Key Students:**
- Belladonna House commoner students (she knows every name, every goal)
- Rosalie von Lichtberg (rare noble who attends, Margaret respects her)
- Students seeking government/corporate positions

**Main Locations:**
- General lecture hall
- Career counseling office (always packed)
- Scarlet Street merchant guild (brings students for networking)

**Teaching Philosophy:**
"The world is unfair. Nobles start ahead. Your job is to run faster, work smarter, and prove them wrong. I'll show you how."

**Judgment Stat:** INT

## Professor Dynamics & Relationships

**Vivienne ↔ Margot**: Mutual professional respect. Both believe in perfection through suffering, though in different domains. Occasionally attend art exhibitions together, criticize everything.

**Robert ↔ Margaret**: Former colleagues in imperial government. Robert mentored Margaret early in her career. Still meet monthly for tea and gossip about economic policy.

**Scar ↔ Vivienne**: Intense mutual dislike. Vivienne thinks she's a "brutish embarrassment to academia." Scar thinks she's "all talk, no spine." Avoid faculty meetings together.

**Lydia ↔ Hemlock**: Research collaborators. Lydia needs alchemical components, Hemlock needs magical theory. Exchange notes via students (neither wants to socialize).

**Margot ↔ Hemlock**: Strange kinship over "beautiful deaths" - she in art critique, she in toxicology. Students find their conversations deeply disturbing.

**Scar ↔ Margaret**: She respects her as "someone who fought their way up." Margaret appreciates her meritocracy. Sometimes spar together (Margaret's bad at it, Scar goes easy, Margaret knows, hates it, keeps trying).

**Robert → All**: Generally well-liked. Even Scar doesn't mind him. "Old man's got sense."

**Lydia**: Avoids all social interaction. Communicates via research notes left in mailboxes.
