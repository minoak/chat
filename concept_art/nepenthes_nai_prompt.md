## Art Style Recommendations
- Visual novel CG quality
- Soft romantic lighting with mysterious undertones
- Detailed eyes showing both warmth and danger
- Elegant noble aesthetic mixed with alchemist elements

## Character Expression Guide
- Default: Gentle half-moon eyes (⌒⌒), soft mysterious smile
- Caring mode: Tilted head, warm embrace, motherly expression
- True nature hints: Smile unchanged but eyes slightly too focused, possessive hand placement
- Happy: Sweet "우후후~" laugh, pleased with collection
- Dangerous: Smile never changes, eyes slightly more intense
